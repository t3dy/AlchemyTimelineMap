Sulfur-Mercury Theory

Category: ACTOR_TERM

The sulfur-mercury theory held that all metals are composed of two elemental principles — sulphur (responsible for combustibility, color, and quality) and mercury (responsible for fusibility, luster, and metallic character) — in varying ratios and degrees of purity. This is an ACTOR_TERM — historical practitioners invoked these principles to explain metallic differences and the possibility of transmutation.
