Iatrochemistry

Category: ANALYST_TERM

Iatrochemistry (from Greek iatros, physician) is a modern scholarly term for the integration of chemical-alchemical practice with medicine, especially in the Paracelsian tradition of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, in which chemical preparations of minerals and metals were advocated as remedies for disease. This is an ANALYST_TERM; practitioners themselves spoke of 'chemical medicine' or 'spagyric art.'
