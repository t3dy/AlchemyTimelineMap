Medicamenta Tria (Three Medicines)

Category: ACTOR_TERM

Tycho Brahe's formulation: three medicinal compounds combining metallic elements and herbal preparations. Inspired by Paracelsian alchemy; focused on medicines not transmutation. Represented systematic, experimental pharmaceutical approach.
