Hermeticism

Category: ANALYST_TERM

Hermetic philosophy derived from texts attributed to Hermes Trismegistus (legendary Egyptian sage). Emphasized divine wisdom, correspondence between microcosm and macrocosm, and the power of human intellect to understand and operate on nature. Central intellectual framework for medieval and early modern alchemy.
