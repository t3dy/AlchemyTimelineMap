Projection

Category: ACTOR_TERM

Projection was the final stage of alchemical transmutation, in which a small quantity of the Philosopher's Stone or prepared tincture was cast onto molten base metal to convert it into gold or silver. This is an ACTOR_TERM: practitioners specifically named this act of casting or projecting the Stone as the culminating act of the Great Work.
