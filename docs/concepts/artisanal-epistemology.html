Artisanal Epistemology

Category: ANALYST_TERM

Modern historiographical term for knowledge produced through craft practice and embodied experience rather than abstract theory or textual study. Pamela Smith's framework emphasizing that medieval and early modern artisans developed sophisticated understanding through doing, not merely following written instructions.
