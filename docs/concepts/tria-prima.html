Tria Prima (Three Principles)

Category: ACTOR_TERM

Tria prima (Latin: three first things) was Paracelsus's revision of elemental theory: all material substances consist of three principles — sulfur (combustibility and soul), mercury (volatility and spirit), and salt (solidity and body). This is an ACTOR_TERM; Paracelsus and his followers explicitly used this framework to explain health, disease, and material transformation.
