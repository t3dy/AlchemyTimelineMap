Putrefaction

Category: ACTOR_TERM

Putrefaction (putrefactio) was an alchemical operation in which a substance was subjected to slow decomposition under controlled conditions — typically moist heat in a sealed vessel — to break down its current form and prepare it for reconstitution in a purer or more elevated state. This is an ACTOR_TERM: historical practitioners recognized and named this operation explicitly.
