Mullite

Category: ACTOR_TERM

High-temperature ceramic material (aluminum silicate compound) formed during firing of clay at intense heat. Medieval and early modern alchemists used mullite-bearing ceramic apparatus (furnaces, retorts, crucibles) to withstand extreme temperatures. Understanding mullite formation (though not the chemistry) was crucial embodied knowledge.
