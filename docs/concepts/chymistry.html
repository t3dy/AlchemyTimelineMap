Chymistry

Category: ANALYST_TERM

Chymistry is a modern historiographical term introduced by William R. Newman and Lawrence M. Principe to refer to the unified practical and theoretical tradition of laboratory chemical investigation in the early modern period (roughly 1400–1750) before the disciplinary separation of alchemy and chemistry. This is an ANALYST_TERM: historical practitioners did not use this label; it is a retrospective scholarly category.
