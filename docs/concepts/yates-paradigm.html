Yates Paradigm

Category: ANALYST_TERM

Frances Yates's influential historiographical framework (late 20th century) emphasizing continuity between Renaissance Hermeticism, alchemy, and early modern intellectual history. Though later scholarship critiqued some claims, Yates demonstrated that magical and esoteric traditions shaped the scientific revolution.
