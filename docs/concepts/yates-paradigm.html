Yates Paradigm

Category: ANALYST_TERM

Frances Yates's thesis (1964) that Renaissance Hermeticism was a catalyst for the Scientific Revolution. Enormously influential but substantially revised by Hanegraaff, Copenhaver, and others who demonstrated Yates overstated the causal link.
