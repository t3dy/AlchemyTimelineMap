Emblematic Alchemy

Category: ANALYST_TERM

Emblematic alchemy is a modern scholarly term for the tradition of alchemical emblem books and illustrated texts — including the Rosarium Philosophorum, Splendor Solis, and Atalanta Fugiens — that communicated alchemical knowledge through sequences of visual images accompanied by explanatory text. This is an ANALYST_TERM for a category of alchemical literature defined by its use of emblematic form.
