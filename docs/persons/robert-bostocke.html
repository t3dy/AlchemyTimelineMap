Robert Bostocke

Role: PHYSICIAN | Era: MEDIEVAL

Robert Bostocke (fl. c. 1585) was an English physician and author of The Difference Betweene the Auncient Phisicke, First Taught by the Godly Forefathers, Consisting in Unite, Peace and Concord: and the Latter Phisicke Proceeding from Idolaters, Ethnickes, and Heathen: as Gallen, and Such Other Consisting in Dualitie, Contrarietie, Strife, and Debate (1585), one of the earliest sustained defenses of Paracelsian medicine in English print. The text argued that Paracelsian chemical medicine represented a return to the true, ancient, divinely revealed medicine of the Old Testament patriarchs, against the corrupted Galenic system introduced by pagan Greek physicians. Bostocke is historically significant as an example of the English reception of Paracelsian Alchemy and as evidence for the religious frameworks through which chemical medicine was legitimized in Elizabethan England.

Literature

Debus, Allen G. The English Paracelsians. Oldbourne, 1965.
Principe, Lawrence M. The Secrets of Alchemy. University of Chicago Press, 2013.
