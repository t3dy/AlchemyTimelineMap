Tycho Brahe

Role: ALCHEMIST | Era: EARLY_MODERN

1546–1601, Danish astronomer and alchemist. Operated sophisticated laboratory at Uraniborg on Ven island, conducting systematic experimental work on metallic compounds and pharmaceutical preparations following Paracelsian principles. Recent excavation and chemical analysis (2024) reveal tungsten and eight other enriched metals in laboratory residues.
