Samuel Norton

Role: ALCHEMIST | Era: EARLY_MODERN

Samuel Norton (1579–1640) was an early modern English alchemist known as 'the English Hermes' who continued and developed the Ripley tradition in the 17th century. Norton's documented experimental work demonstrates how the English systematic approach persisted and evolved through hands-on laboratory practice, maintaining the integration of theory and hands-on experimentation characteristic of the tradition.

Norton's continuation of the Ripley tradition in practical form demonstrates the vitality of English alchemy through the 16th and 17th centuries, showing how medieval systematization could be maintained and refined through ongoing laboratory engagement.
