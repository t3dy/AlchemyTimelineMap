Elias Ashmole

Role: SCHOLAR | Era: EARLY_MODERN

Elias Ashmole (1617–1692) was an English antiquarian, collector, and alchemist who played a crucial role in preserving and disseminating the Ripley corpus in manuscript form. His collections, now in the Bodleian Library at Oxford, contain numerous copies of Ripley texts representing the continuous transmission of English alchemical manuscripts from medieval through early modern periods.

Ashmole's work demonstrates the continuity of the Ripley tradition into the 17th century, showing how medieval and Renaissance teachings remained living practices for early modern scholars.
