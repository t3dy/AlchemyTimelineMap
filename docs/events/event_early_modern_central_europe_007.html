Scholarly Context

Paula Findlen demonstrated that Vienna's imperial court patronage supported alchemical research as part of the Kunstkammer tradition in *Possessing Nature* (1994) ch.6.

c. 1542, Vienna: Alchemical practitioners and physicians in Vienna's growing laboratory culture pursue investigations into the separation and recombination of materials through distillation and fermentation, establishing techniques that would later influence the chemical revolution. The Vienna tradition of empirical alchemy in the 1540s maintained continuous engagement with practical laboratory operations. This period established Vienna as a rival center to Prague for alchemical patronage and practice within Central European Holy Roman Empire territories.
