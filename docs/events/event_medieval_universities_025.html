c. 1500 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Paris

France

c. 1500 CE, Paris: The University of Paris enters the early modern period with its tradition of integrating Operational Chemistry and natural-philosophy fully established in the curriculum of the Faculty of Arts and the Faculty of Medicine. The comprehensive integration of alchemical knowledge into university instruction that began in the thirteenth century has, by 1500, produced generations of scholars trained in both the theory and practice of alchemy. Paris's role in establishing alchemy as a legitimate and permanent feature of European university education ensures that the transition to the early modern period is marked by continuity in alchemical study rather than rupture.
