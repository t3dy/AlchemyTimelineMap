c. 1578 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Vienna

Austria

c. 1578, Vienna: Vienna's imperial patronage of alchemical research reaches a peak as Emperor Rudolf II establishes his legendary Kunstkammer and laboratory, attracting Europe's most accomplished alchemists, metallurgists, and natural philosophers. Vienna's chemical laboratories of this period represent the institutional consolidation of transmutation research. This moment marks Vienna's emergence as the epicenter of imperial alchemical patronage that would define Central European chemistry through the early seventeenth century.
