Prague

Bohemia

c. 1476 CE, Prague: The University of Prague's natural philosophy faculty consolidates its reputation as a leading European center for the study of Operational Chemistry, with the university maintaining extensive collections of alchemical texts and training generations of scholars in both theoretical understanding and practical knowledge. Prague's success in establishing itself as an intellectual peer to Paris, Oxford, and Bologna demonstrates that universities beyond the traditional centers could achieve excellence in natural philosophy and its alchemical applications. Prague's intellectual achievement validates the model of distributed alchemical learning across multiple European universities.
