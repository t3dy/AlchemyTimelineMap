Scholarly Context

Deborah Harkness demonstrated that early modern London had active networks of alchemists and chemists pursuing practical experimentation in *The Jewel House* (2007) ch.7.

c. 1596, London: English physicians and surgeons integrate alchemical mineral medicines into their practice with increasing confidence, having established through repeated experiment that Operational Chemistry produces medically efficacious compounds. The antimony-based and mercury-based medicines derived from alchemical operations, despite occasional toxicity, demonstrate clear therapeutic effects that conventional Galenic pharmacy cannot match. This clinical validation of alchemical medicine enhances the social standing of practitioners in England.
