c. 1316 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Florence

Italy

c. 1316, Florence: The Florentine Dominican friar Giordano da Pisa mentions alchemical Transmutation and gold-making in his public sermons, demonstrating that alchemical theory has penetrated the discourse of the mendicant orders and reached educated clergy. His references to Quintessence and the secrets of metals reflect the consolidation of medieval Arabic alchemical knowledge in European theological and philosophical circles. This sermon record testifies to how thoroughly Operational Chemistry has ceased to be an exotic Eastern curiosity and has become a legitimate subject of clerical intellectual inquiry in Renaissance Florence.

Concepts

  • Transmutation
  • Quintessence
  • Operational Chemistry
