c. 976 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Ray (Rhages)

Persia

c. 976 CE, Rayy: The continuation of operative chemistry in Rayy reflects the deep entrenchment of alchemical practice in Islamic natural philosophy. Practitioners refine and document techniques of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination grounded in the methodologies established by earlier masters. Rayy's alchemical schools maintain laboratories equipped with sophisticated apparatus for these operations. The standardization and codification of procedure evident in Rayy's alchemical culture constitute a level of technical sophistication that medieval European alchemists will gradually assimilate through translation and scholarly exchange.

Concepts

  • Distillation
  • Sublimation
  • Calcination
