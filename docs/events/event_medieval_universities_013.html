c. 1356 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Paris

France

c. 1356 CE, Paris: The University of Paris maintains its position as the leading center for natural philosophical study in Latin Christendom, with the Faculty of Arts incorporating systematic analysis of Operational Chemistry and natural-philosophy. By the mid-fourteenth century, alchemical operations are so thoroughly integrated into natural philosophy teaching that they are routinely discussed alongside Aristotelian accounts of elemental transformation and the properties of matter. The mature integration of alchemical knowledge into the Parisian curriculum reflects decades of successful transmission of Islamic scientific and alchemical texts into Latin European contexts. Paris's role in normalizing alchemical study within university natural philosophy ensures alchemy's legitimacy in the European intellectual tradition.
