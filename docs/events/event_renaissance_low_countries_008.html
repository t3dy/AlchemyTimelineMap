c. 1464 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Antwerp

Low Countries

c. 1464, Antwerp: Antwerp's development as a center for luxury goods production—including pharmaceuticals, dyes, and cosmetics—created intensive demand for practitioners skilled in Distillation and chemical-experimentation. The city's guild system provided organizational structures that transmitted practical knowledge across generations while maintaining quality standards. This period marks the emergence of alchemy as integrated craft knowledge rather than isolated scholarly pursuit or magical ideology.
