c. 1674 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Paris

France

c. 1674, Paris: The later seventeenth century witnesses continuing Parisian interest in preserving and cataloging alchemical manuscripts and treatises even as active alchemical research declines. Libraries and collectors maintain substantial holdings of chemical-practice texts, recognizing their value for understanding both the history of learning and the development of practical chemistry. The preservation of alchemical literature testifies to acknowledgment of alchemy's historical importance even as its contemporary relevance diminishes. The late seventeenth-century cataloging and preservation of alchemical texts in Paris secured these works for modern historical and scientific study.
