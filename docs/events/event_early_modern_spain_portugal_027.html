Scholarly Context

Deborah Harkness demonstrated that Lisbon was a crucial hub for the circulation of alchemical knowledge through maritime trade and printing in early modern Europe in *The Jewel House* (2007) ch.6.

c. 1662 CE, Lisbon: The Kitāb al-Ḥāsib, the foundational Arab alchemical treatise, appears in new scholarly editions circulating among Portuguese researchers. This direct engagement with Arabic alchemical sources reinforces Lisbon's cosmopolitan intellectual identity and its commitment to the full range of alchemical traditions. Portuguese scholars recognize in Jabir's systematic treatment of Operational Chemistry the empirical grounding necessary to distinguish genuine chemical knowledge from mystical speculation. The continued circulation of medieval Arab alchemy validates Portuguese alchemists' claims to stand within an unbroken tradition of rigorous material investigation.
