c. 1452 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Paris

France

c. 1452 CE, Paris: Scholars at Paris encounter and begin teaching from the Liber XXIV Philosophorum (Book of Twenty-Four Philosophers), a Latin compilation of twenty-four philosophical principles and their practical applications, which provides a systematic philosophical foundation for understanding Operational Chemistry and the transformation of matter. The Liber XXIV Philosophorum offers Paris scholars a text that integrates metaphysical principle with practical application, satisfying the university's tradition of combining abstract natural philosophy with concrete alchemical instruction. The circulation of this text within the Parisian curriculum reflects the university's commitment to providing both theoretical justification and practical knowledge in alchemical studies.
