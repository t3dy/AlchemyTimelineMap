c. 952 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Ray (Rhages)

Persia

Scholarly Context

Wouter Hanegraaff demonstrated that alchemical knowledge transmission involved both written texts and embodied apprenticeship in *Western Esotericism* (2012) ch.4.

c. 952 CE, Rayy: The Liber XXIV Philosophorum (Book of Twenty-Four Philosophers), a comprehensive philosophical text incorporating alchemical teachings, circulates among Rayy's intellectual elite. This work synthesizes the achievements of earlier alchemists including Jabir ibn Hayyan and Al-Razi (Rhazes), presenting Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination within a broader cosmological framework. The Liber XXIV Philosophorum represents the maturation of Islamic alchemy into a sophisticated natural philosophy integrated with metaphysics. Its eventual circulation in medieval Europe will influence alchemists' understanding of the cosmic significance of chemical operations.
