c. 1566 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Vienna

Austria

c. 1566, Vienna: Vienna's alchemical practitioners continue their pursuit of transmutation through laboratory operations while maintaining integration with the medical tradition established by Paracelsus. The Vienna chemical tradition of this period maintained the connection between alchemical theory and practical medicine. Vienna's investment in laboratory infrastructure and personnel established continuity of chemical knowledge through the period of religious conflict that would follow.
