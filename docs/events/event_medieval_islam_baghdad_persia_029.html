c. 1048 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Ray (Rhages)

Persia

c. 1048 CE, Rayy: The continuation of alchemical learning in Rayy during the eleventh century reflects the discipline's institutional maturity. Practitioners maintain the legacy of Al-Razi (Rhazes) and Jabir ibn Hayyan, investigating the fundamental principles governing matter transformation through Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination. Rayy's alchemical schools possess comprehensive knowledge of apparatus design, material purification, and operational precision. This accumulated expertise constitutes the highest level of chemical knowledge in the medieval world.

Concepts

  • Distillation
  • Sublimation
  • Calcination
