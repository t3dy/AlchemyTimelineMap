Scholarly Context

Danielle Jacquart and Francoise Micheau showed transmutation aspirations structured Arabic alchemical practice and medicine in *The Natural History of the Universe in Arabic Sources* (2008) ch.5.

c. 1638, Paris: The accelerating publication of Cartesian and Baconian natural philosophy in early seventeenth-century France provides new intellectual frameworks that challenge alchemical assumptions about matter, causation, and human knowledge. Parisian intellectuals debate whether Transmutation remains compatible with the new mechanical natural philosophy or whether alchemy belongs to a superseded worldview. The legitimacy of chemical-practice as a knowledge source becomes contestable within mainstream natural philosophy, though laboratory work continues. The conflict between alchemical and mechanical frameworks in mid-seventeenth-century Paris marks a crucial moment in the scientific transformation of chemistry.
