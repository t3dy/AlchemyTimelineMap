Prague

Bohemia

c. 1332 CE, Prague: Prague's Natural Philosophy faculty maintains its reputation as a center for empirical investigation into Operational Chemistry, attracting students and scholars seeking training in the full range of alchemical operations from across Germanic and Slavic regions. The university's integration of practical alchemical training with abstract natural philosophy instruction creates a distinctive educational model that values both theoretical understanding and experimental verification. Prague's success as a center for alchemical education demonstrates that universities beyond the established intellectual centers of Paris, Oxford, and Bologna could achieve recognition for excellence in natural philosophy and its alchemical applications.
