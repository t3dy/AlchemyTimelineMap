Scholarly Context

Lawrence Principe showed medieval distillation techniques developed from Greek and Arabic alchemical practice into a sophisticated pharmaceutical and transmutational technology in *The Secrets of Alchemy* (2013) ch.2.

c. 630 CE, Ray (Persia): The physician and alchemist Al-Razi (Rhazes) composes treatises on Distillation and pharmaceutical preparation, establishing the foundations of systematic Operational Chemistry in the Islamic world. Al-Razi's works document the practical production of sulfuric acid, alcohol, and other corrosive substances through precise distillation methods, marking a watershed in the transition from metallurgical craft to repeatable chemical operation. His techniques and theoretical framework, transmitted through subsequent Arab alchemists and later translated into Latin, become foundational to European alchemy for centuries to come.
