Scholarly Context

Allen Debus showed the early modern period witnessed the gradual transformation of alchemy into chymistry and chemical medicine in *The Chemical Philosophy* (1977) vol.1 ch.1.

c. 1516, Venice: A Venetian printer produces the first printed edition of Kitāb al-Ḥāsib, attributed to Jabir ibn Hayyan, in Latin translation, making Jabir's foundational Operational Chemistry texts available to a broad European readership. This publication event demonstrates Venice's growing role as a printing center for alchemical and scientific texts, rivaling Florence in the mass production and distribution of specialized knowledge. The widespread circulation of Jabir's protocols accelerates the transition from theoretical speculation to standardized experimental practice.
