c. 1348 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Florence

Italy

c. 1348, Florence: As the Black Death devastates Europe, a Florentine physician compiles a medical treatise incorporating alchemical Quintessence preparations and herbal distillates from De Sex Rerum Principiis (On the Six Principles of Things). His work represents an attempt to weaponize Operational Chemistry against plague, drawing on the assumption that distilled essences and Transmutation of base medicines into pure quintessences might restore health. This pandemic-driven synthesis of alchemical theory and medical practice demonstrates how crisis conditions propel the practical application of alchemy in Renaissance society.
