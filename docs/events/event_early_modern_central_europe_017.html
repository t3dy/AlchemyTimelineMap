c. 1602 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Vienna

Austria

c. 1602, Vienna: Vienna maintains vigorous alchemical research and laboratory practice under imperial patronage, preserving institutional commitment to chemical operations and transmutation theory during the period preceding the Thirty Years War. Vienna's alchemical tradition of the early seventeenth century sustained the continuity of medieval and early modern operational knowledge. This period established lasting traditions of chemical practice that would survive even the religious and political upheaval of the coming decades.
