Madrid

Iberia

c. 1692 CE, Madrid: Spanish natural philosophers increasingly resist the alchemy label while defending the legitimacy and value of Operational Chemistry. Distillation, crystallization, and metal-refining are reframed as chemical operations pursued through experimental method rather than alchemical theory. The theoretical goal of Transmutation is quietly abandoned or relegated to historical curiosity, while the practical operations that once served transmutational ambitions are preserved and systematized. Madrid exemplifies alchemy's gradual mutation into chemistry, a process of theoretical abandonment coupled with operational continuity.
