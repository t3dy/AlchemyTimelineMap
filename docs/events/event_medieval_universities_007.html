Scholarly Context

Wouter Hanegraaff showed late medieval alchemical literature merged practical laboratory operations with mystical and hermetic philosophy in *Western Esotericism* (2012) ch.3.

c. 1284 CE, Prague: The University of Prague's natural philosophy faculty consolidates its reputation for rigorous teaching in Operational Chemistry and natural-philosophy, attracting scholars from across Central and Northern Europe who study the full range of alchemical operations—distillation, calcination, crystallization—within a framework emphasizing empirical verification and logical reasoning. Prague's reputation for combining Baconian empiricism with systematic Aristotelian natural philosophy makes it a leading center for alchemical education in medieval Europe. The university's success in integrating alchemy into natural philosophy demonstrates that Northern European universities could match the intellectual achievement of the established centers at Paris, Oxford, and Bologna.
