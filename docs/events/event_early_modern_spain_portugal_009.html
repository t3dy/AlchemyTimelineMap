Lisbon

Iberia

c. 1554 CE, Lisbon: The Emerald Tablet (Tabula Smaragdina), the most famous alchemical text in the Western tradition, appears in Portuguese manuscripts and scholarly collections, circulating in Latin translation among university scholars and private collectors. This cryptic statement of hermetic philosophy—"That which is below is like that which is above"—provides Portuguese alchemists with a metaphysical anchor for understanding Operational Chemistry as microcosmic reflection of cosmic transformation. Scholars debate the tablet's proper interpretation, oscillating between mystical-allegorical and practical-chemical readings. The Emerald Tablet's presence in Lisbon's libraries signals the city's incorporation into international networks of alchemical transmission and validates the pursuit of Transmutation as respectable intellectual work.
