c. 1416 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Antwerp

Low Countries

c. 1416, Antwerp: Antwerp's guild structures and craft traditions provided fertile ground for the consolidation of alchemical knowledge transmitted from medieval sources. The systematic study of Distillation and chemical-experimentation in Antwerp's artisanal workshops reflects the city's emergence as a nexus for practical chemistry informed by theory. This event represents the integration of alchemy into the organized craft economy of the Low Countries, a development distinct from both the isolated mysticism of earlier traditions and the later mathematical precision of early modern science.
