c. 1652 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Florence

Italy

c. 1652, Florence: The Grand Ducal laboratory, having operated continuously for nearly a century, closes as new chemical and mechanical philosophies render the hermetic-alchemical synthesis increasingly marginal to elite intellectual discourse. The closure marks the symbolic end of the Renaissance-Enlightenment transition in alchemical thought, though Operational Chemistry techniques and Transmutation theory continue to influence practitioners beyond the formal scientific establishment.

Concepts

  • Transmutation
  • Quintessence
  • Operational Chemistry
