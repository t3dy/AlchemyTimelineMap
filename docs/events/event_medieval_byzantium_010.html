c. 900 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Constantinople

Byzantium

c. 900 CE, Baghdad: Alchemical scholarship in the Islamic world reaches a mature synthesis of Operational Chemistry and Hermetic philosophy, with elaborate theoretical justifications for Distillation and transmutational operations. The production of comprehensive alchemical encyclopedias reflects the institutionalization of alchemy within courtly and scholarly networks. These works, preserved in monastery libraries and cathedral schools, will constitute the primary sources available to medieval European alchemists.
