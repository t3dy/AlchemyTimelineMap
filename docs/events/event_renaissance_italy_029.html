c. 1532 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Venice

Italy

c. 1532, Venice: The Venetian state establishes a school for metallurgists and assayers, formally training practitioners in the application of Operational Chemistry to the testing and refinement of precious metals. This institutional commitment to training in alchemical operations reflects Venice's long-term investment in maintaining technological and economic advantage through systematic knowledge transmission. The school becomes a magnet for aspiring practitioners from across Europe seeking certification in advanced techniques.
