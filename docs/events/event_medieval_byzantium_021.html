Constantinople

Byzantium

c. 1230 CE, Oxford: The Liber XXIV Philosophorum (Book of Twenty-Four Philosophers) circulates in Latin translation, offering a systematic metaphysical framework for understanding Operational Chemistry and transmutational theory. This text synthesizes Neoplatonic and Hermetic philosophy with practical alchemical claims, providing intellectual justification for the pursuit of transmutation alongside legitimation of Distillation and other chemical operations. The Liber becomes canonical in European alchemical curricula.
