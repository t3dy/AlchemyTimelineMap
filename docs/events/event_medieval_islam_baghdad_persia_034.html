Baghdad

Iraq

c. 1108 CE, Baghdad: The maturity of Islamic alchemical tradition in Baghdad at the turn of the twelfth century reflects four centuries of accumulated expertise. Scholars and practitioners maintain sophisticated understanding of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination, grounded in both theoretical sophistication and practical mastery. Baghdad's position as a global center of learning ensures that alchemical knowledge is preserved and transmitted across the Islamic world. The discipline's institutional maturity and intellectual prestige position it for eventual transmission to medieval Europe through translation and scholarly exchange.
