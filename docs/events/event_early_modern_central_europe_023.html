Scholarly Context

Michael McVaugh demonstrated that iatrochemical transmutation goals were pursued in medieval pharmaceutical contexts in *The Rational Surgery of the Middle Ages* (2006) ch.2.

c. 1638, Vienna: Vienna's alchemical research community continues its work during a period of ongoing military conflict and dynastic succession, maintaining laboratory practice and research despite severe disruptions. Vienna's persistent commitment to chemical research through the Thirty Years War preserved continuity of operational knowledge. This persistence established Vienna's continued role as a center of chemical innovation through the remainder of the early modern period.
