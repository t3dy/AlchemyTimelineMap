c. 1692 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

London

England

c. 1692, London: As the century draws to a close, alchemy's status in English thought has become definitively historical and antiquarian. Alchemical texts are collected and studied by natural philosophers like Newton not as guides to current inquiry but as evidence of earlier attempts to understand matter. The techniques of Operational Chemistry have been naturalized within the new discipline of chemistry without acknowledgment of their alchemical origins. Alchemy has become simultaneously invisible and foundational to English natural philosophy.
