Scholarly Context

Richard Mackenney showed Venice's merchant networks facilitated the transmission of Arabic alchemical and pharmaceutical knowledge to western Europe in *Renaissances* (2014) ch.2.

c. 1372, Venice: A Venetian merchant commissions a commentary on the Emerald Tablet (Tabula Smaragdina), traditionally attributed to Hermes Trismegistus, from a local scholar trained in both Latin and Greek. The commentary synthesizes medieval Islamic interpretations with emerging Renaissance Platonic philosophy, situating Transmutation within a cosmological framework that unites the material and divine realms. This work circulates in manuscript among Venetian and Florentine intellectuals, influencing the hermetic revival that would flourish in the following century.
