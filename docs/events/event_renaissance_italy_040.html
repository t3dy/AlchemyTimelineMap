Scholarly Context

Michael McVaugh demonstrated that iatrochemical transmutation goals were pursued in medieval pharmaceutical contexts in *The Rational Surgery of the Middle Ages* (2006) ch.2.

c. 1620, Florence: The Grand Ducal laboratory continues systematic research into mineral transformation and metal refinement, now employing increasingly sophisticated experimental protocols derived from both alchemical tradition and emerging chemical theory. The synthesis of alchemical Operational Chemistry with early modern chemical methodology demonstrates how hermetic and Islamic traditions undergo creative transformation within the Italian scientific context.
