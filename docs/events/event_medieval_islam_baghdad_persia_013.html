Scholarly Context

Lawrence Principe showed Roger Bacon distinguished legitimate experimental alchemy from fraudulent gold-making, defending transmutation's theoretical possibility in *The Secrets of Alchemy* (2013) pp.57-63.

c. 856 CE, Rayy: The scholar Roger Bacon, though born centuries later, inherits the entire intellectual tradition of Islamic alchemy centered in Rayy and Baghdad. By this date, the systematic treatises of Jabir ibn Hayyan and Al-Razi (Rhazes) on Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination have achieved canonical status, copied and studied across the Islamic world. The methodological rigor established in Rayy becomes the foundation upon which Roger Bacon will build his own alchemical philosophy and experimental program in medieval Oxford.
