Madrid

Iberia

c. 1680 CE, Madrid: The medieval text De Sex Rerum Principiis (On the Six Principles of Things), once central to alchemical theory, becomes primarily of historical interest to Madrid's scholars. The six-principle theory of matter gives way to more empirically grounded frameworks for understanding chemical composition. Yet Spanish chemists continue to practice the very operations the medieval text described—distillation, sublimation, crystallization—now understood through mechanical-corpuscular theory. Madrid's alchemists exemplify the gradual decoupling of operations from theory: Operational Chemistry survives its theoretical underpinnings and transforms into experimental chemistry.
