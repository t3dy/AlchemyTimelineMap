Scholarly Context

Michael McVaugh demonstrated that iatrochemical transmutation goals were pursued in medieval pharmaceutical contexts in *The Rational Surgery of the Middle Ages* (2006) ch.2.

c. 1692, Prague: Prague's alchemical community continues its investigations into transmutation and material transformation as the early modern period approaches its conclusion. Prague's continued engagement with alchemical operations reflects the slow transition from alchemy to chemistry as the theoretical framework shifted. Prague's laboratories remained important centers of practical chemical investigation despite changing philosophical assumptions.
