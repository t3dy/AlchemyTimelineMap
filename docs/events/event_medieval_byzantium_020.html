Byzantium

c. 1200 CE, Bologna: Alchemical operations, particularly Distillation and the production of mineral acids, become standard elements of apothecaries' and physicians' practical training. The legitimacy conferred by university incorporation and the circulation of authoritative translated texts normalize alchemical practice within medical and pharmaceutical contexts. Operational Chemistry becomes recognized as integral to the care of bodies and the preparation of medicines.
