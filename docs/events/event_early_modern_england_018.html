c. 1608 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

London

England

c. 1608, London: The foundational alchemical treatise Summa Perfectionis (Sum of Perfection), attributed in the English tradition to Jabir ibn Hayyan, receives fresh study among English natural philosophers intent on systematizing the principles of metallurgical Operational Chemistry. The Summa's careful exposition of distillation apparatus, acid production, and metal-working techniques provides an intellectual framework that English practitioners adopt for their own operations. This marks the full integration of Jabirian alchemy into English natural philosophy.
