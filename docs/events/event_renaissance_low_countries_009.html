Bruges

Low Countries

c. 1472, Bruges: The widespread circulation of the Emerald Tablet (Tabula Smaragdina) (Tabula Smaragdina), the foundational hermetic text known in multiple Latin and Arabic recensions, shaped Bruges' alchemical intellectual environment during the late 15th century. This text's aphoristic account of transformation and the unity of macrocosm and microcosm provided theoretical justification for experimental Distillation and chemical-experimentation. The Emerald Tablet's enduring influence established a consistent framework connecting mystical philosophy to practical operations.
