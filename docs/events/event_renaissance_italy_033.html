c. 1564 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Venice

Italy

c. 1564, Venice: A Venetian scholar publishes a comprehensive concordance to the Emerald Tablet (Tabula Smaragdina), gathering interpretations from Islamic, medieval European, and contemporary Renaissance commentators on the text's meaning and application to Transmutation practice. This reference work demonstrates the mature Renaissance scholarship applied to alchemical canonical texts, synthesizing centuries of interpretation into a single usable resource. The concordance facilitates rapid cross-referencing among practitioners seeking the hermetic roots of their operations.
