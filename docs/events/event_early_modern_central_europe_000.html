Prague

Bohemia

c. 1500, Prague: The Danish astronomer and alchemist Tycho Brahe, whose alchemical interests are documented in contemporary letters and manuscript annotations, engages with Corpus Hermeticum in the context of early modern Prague's growing interest in Hermetic philosophy and astronomical-alchemical correspondences. Tycho's laboratory work combined observational astronomy with alchemical operations, reflecting the intellectual synthesis characteristic of late Renaissance natural philosophy. His synthesis of empirical observation with alchemical theory marks a critical moment in the transition from medieval alchemy toward the mathematical and experimental approaches that would dominate seventeenth-century chemistry.
