c. 1440 CE, Bologna: Bologna's faculty of medicine and natural philosophy continues to emphasize the practical value of Operational Chemistry for pharmaceutical preparation and medical practice, integrating alchemical techniques into the standard medical curriculum. By the mid-fifteenth century, the connection between alchemical operations and medical pharmacy is so well established that medical students routinely study distillation, sublimation, and other operations as essential professional skills. Bologna's success in integrating alchemical knowledge into professional medical training demonstrates the practical utility of alchemy beyond theoretical natural philosophy.
