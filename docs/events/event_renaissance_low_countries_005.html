c. 1440 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Bruges

Low Countries

c. 1440, Bruges: The mid-15th century saw Bruges consolidating its role as a center for manuscript production and intellectual exchange, with alchemical texts among the works copied and studied. The systematic engagement with Distillation and chemical-experimentation during this period reflects the integration of practical and theoretical alchemy within the city's established scholarly networks. Bruges' position as a diplomatic and commercial hub ensured that such knowledge circulated beyond the Low Countries to England and the Hanseatic League.
