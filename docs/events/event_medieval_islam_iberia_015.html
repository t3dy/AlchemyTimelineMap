c. 980 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Toledo

Spain

c. 980 CE, Toledo: The manuscript known as Ms. Fr. 640 (Anonymous Technical Manuscript), a technical compilation of alchemical procedures, experimental methods, and theoretical instruction, reflects the remarkably sophisticated state of Iberian alchemical knowledge achieved by the late tenth century. Though formally dated to the 1580s, its substantive content derives directly from earlier Andalusian sources, preserving detailed techniques in Distillation, advanced Fermentation, and Transmutation developed through centuries of continuous experimentation. Toledo's well-maintained libraries contain copies of such comprehensive manuscripts, representing the cumulative knowledge inherited from eastern Islamic tradition and decades of Iberian innovation. The deliberate preservation of these detailed technical documents ensures practical continuity of sophisticated operations even as broader political circumstances change significantly. Technical knowledge becomes embedded in durable texts.
