Prague

Bohemia

c. 1572, Prague: The Picatrix (Ghāyat al-Hakīm), a medieval Arabic magical text on astrological operations and cosmic correspondences, circulates among Prague's intellectual elite, providing theoretical justification for understanding transmutation as an operation deriving from celestial and planetary influences. The Picatrix tradition in Prague linked alchemical operations to planetary hours and correspondences, establishing a comprehensive philosophy of nature. This text's circulation in Prague established the framework for understanding transmutation as a consequence of cosmic harmony rather than mechanical accident.
