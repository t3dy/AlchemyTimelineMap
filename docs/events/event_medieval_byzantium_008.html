Constantinople

Byzantium

c. 840 CE, Baghdad: Alchemical works compiled and circulated from the House of Wisdom document advances in Distillation apparatus design and Operational Chemistry methodology. The precision of these technical descriptions, supported by mathematical and philosophical argumentation, reflects the integration of alchemy into the broader program of natural philosophy pursued under Abbasid patronage. These texts, eventually translated into Latin, will provide Western alchemists with detailed operational protocols.
