Bruges

Low Countries

c. 1568, Bruges: The later 16th century saw Bruges preserve an important historical archive of alchemical manuscripts and knowledge even as the city's economic and intellectual prominence declined. The study of Liber XXIV Philosophorum (Book of Twenty-Four Philosophers) (Book of Twenty-Four Philosophers), a 12th-century text on being and causation, provided theoretical frameworks supporting practitioners' work in Distillation and chemical-experimentation. The text's emphasis on the unity of principles undergirded both mystical philosophy and practical operations.
