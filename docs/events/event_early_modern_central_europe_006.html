c. 1536 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Prague

Bohemia

c. 1536, Prague: The circulation of the De Sex Rerum Principiis (On the Six Principles of Things), an important medieval alchemical treatise on the six principles (salt, sulphur, mercury, and three others), continues in manuscript form among Prague scholars and laboratory practitioners. This text's continued importance in Prague reflects the medieval continuity of alchemical theory into the early modern period. The Prague manuscript tradition of medieval alchemical texts preserved operational knowledge that would otherwise have been lost to the mechanical philosophy of the seventeenth century.
