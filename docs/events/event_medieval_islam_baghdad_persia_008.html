Scholarly Context

Lawrence Principe showed Roger Bacon distinguished legitimate experimental alchemy from fraudulent gold-making, defending transmutation's theoretical possibility in *The Secrets of Alchemy* (2013) pp.57-63.

c. 796 CE, Baghdad: The scholar Roger Bacon, though primarily active in later medieval Oxford, represents the eventual culmination of transmission from Baghdad's alchemical tradition. During this period, Baghdad remains the preeminent center of alchemical learning, where Jabir ibn Hayyan and Al-Razi (Rhazes) have established methodologies of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination as standard practices. The intellectual mechanisms enabling scholars like Roger Bacon to inherit and develop this knowledge are being established through written transmission and scholarly networks.
