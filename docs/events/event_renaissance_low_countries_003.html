c. 1424 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Bruges

Low Countries

c. 1424, Bruges: Bruges' manuscript collections grew substantially in the early 15th century, including copies of the Kitāb al-Ḥāsib (attributed to Jabir ibn Hayyan), a foundational text on practical alchemical operations. The presence of this Arabic-Latin text in Bruges demonstrates how medieval translation networks continued to enrich northern European alchemical studies well into the Renaissance. The work's detailed accounts of Distillation and metal chemistry provided operational frameworks that influenced local practitioners engaging in chemical-experimentation.
