Antwerp

Low Countries

c. 1544, Antwerp: Antwerp's dominance as a center for practical alchemy reached its height during the mid-16th century, with the city hosting practitioners, patrons, and scholars from across Europe. The circulation of Summa Perfectionis (Sum of Perfection) (Sum of Perfection, attributed to Jabir ibn Hayyan), a comprehensive technical manual on metallurgical and chemical operations, provided authoritative frameworks for the systematic Distillation and chemical-experimentation that defined Antwerp's reputation.
