Allen Debus showed the early modern period witnessed the gradual transformation of alchemy into chymistry and chemical medicine in *The Chemical Philosophy* (1977) vol.1 ch.1.

c. 1524 CE, Prague: Scholars at Prague study and teach from the Kitāb al-Ḥāsib, continuing the university's tradition of grounding alchemical instruction in the most authoritative Arabic sources available through Latin translation. The sustained engagement with Jabir ibn Hayyan's systematic treatments of Operational Chemistry ensures that Prague's scholars maintain direct connection to the foundational texts and methods that have shaped European alchemy since its transmission from the Islamic world. Prague's continued study of the foundational sources demonstrates the university's commitment to rigorous alchemical education grounded in authoritative texts.
