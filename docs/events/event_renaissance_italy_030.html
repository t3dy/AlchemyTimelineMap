Scholarly Context

Andrew Pettegree showed that print culture was central to the dissemination of alchemical texts in early modern Europe in *The Book in the Renaissance* (2010) ch.4.

c. 1540, Florence: A Florentine printer produces a newly edited and annotated edition of De Sex Rerum Principiis (On the Six Principles of Things), incorporating the latest Renaissance interpretations of medieval alchemical philosophy and its cosmological implications for Transmutation theory. The critical apparatus surrounding this text demonstrates Florence's capacity for sophisticated textual scholarship applied to alchemical sources, establishing editorial and hermeneutic standards that would influence European alchemical study for centuries.
