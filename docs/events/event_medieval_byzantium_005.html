Scholarly Context

Garth Fowden situated Constantinople as a bridge between Greek and Islamic alchemical traditions in *The Egyptian Hermes* (1986) ch.6.

c. 750 CE, Baghdad: The Abbasid court at Baghdad emerges as a major center of alchemical and pharmaceutical scholarship, with scholars systematizing Operational Chemistry and Distillation techniques. The establishment of royal patronage for alchemical research, particularly in the context of pharmacological innovation and mineral processing, transforms alchemy from dispersed craft knowledge into institutionalized natural philosophy. This shift creates the conditions for the production of comprehensive theoretical treatises and the emergence of alchemy as a legitimate field of inquiry.
