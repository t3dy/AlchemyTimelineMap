Scholarly Context

Leah DeVun showed transmutation was pursued within mystical and theological frameworks by medieval and early modern alchemists in *Prophecy, Alchemy, and the End of Time* (2020) ch.4.

c. 1492, Florence: The Florentine humanist and patron of learning Lorenzo de' Medici commissions a comprehensive Latin translation and commentary on the Corpus Hermeticum, synthesizing decades of Florentine Neoplatonic and alchemical scholarship into a definitive reference work on hermetic philosophy and Transmutation theory. This monumental effort consolidates Florence's intellectual supremacy in the hermetic revival and ensures that the city remains the epicenter of Western engagement with Hermes Trismegistus and his alchemical legacy.
