Scholarly Context

Allen Debus showed the early modern period witnessed the gradual transformation of alchemy into chymistry and chemical medicine in *The Chemical Philosophy* (1977) vol.1 ch.1.

c. 1524, Florence: The Florentine physician and alchemist Giovan Battista della Porta begins his systematic studies of natural magic and Transmutation, drawing on the full corpus of hermetic, alchemical, and Islamic learning available in Florence's libraries. His work exemplifies the mature Renaissance integration of alchemical theory with natural philosophy, treating Quintessence and Operational Chemistry as legitimate subjects of rigorous scientific inquiry conducted within a Christian intellectual framework.
