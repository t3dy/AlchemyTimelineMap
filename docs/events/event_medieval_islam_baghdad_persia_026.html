Scholarly Context

Lawrence Principe showed sublimation was used to purify and concentrate volatile components in both pharmaceutical preparation and transmutational operations in *The Secrets of Alchemy* (2013) pp.18-22.

c. 1012 CE, Baghdad: The transition to the eleventh century finds Baghdad maintaining its scholarly commitment to alchemical investigation. The foundational works of Jabir ibn Hayyan remain canonical, studied by successive generations of natural philosophers. Baghdad's alchemists continue to refine techniques of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination, extending the experimental legacy established centuries earlier. The intellectual climate supporting rigorous investigation of material transformation persists, preserving for future transmission the accumulated wisdom of Islamic chemistry.
