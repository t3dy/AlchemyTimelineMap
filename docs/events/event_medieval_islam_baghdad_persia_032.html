Baghdad

Iraq

c. 1084 CE, Baghdad: Baghdad's alchemical and philosophical traditions persist into the late eleventh century, maintaining the standards of rigor established by Jabir ibn Hayyan and Al-Razi (Rhazes). The investigation of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination continues to occupy natural philosophers and practicing alchemists. Baghdad's libraries preserve the canonical texts of Islamic alchemy, ensuring that knowledge accumulated over four centuries remains accessible to successive generations of scholars. The intellectual infrastructure supporting advanced chemical investigation remains intact.

  • Calcination
