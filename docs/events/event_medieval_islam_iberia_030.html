c. 1160 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Córdoba

Spain

c. 1160 CE, Córdoba: The De Sex Rerum Principiis (On the Six Principles of Things) (On the Six Principles of Things), still actively circulating in Cordoban libraries and studied by Christian, Muslim, and Jewish scholars, provides sophisticated philosophical grounding for understanding Distillation, Fermentation, and the theoretical possibility of Transmutation. Cordoban scholars of this period, working across religious communities, actively engage with this text's complex arguments about substance, transformation, and material reality as part of their regular intellectual practice. The text represents the philosophical synthesis that characterizes mature Islamic alchemy—rigorous, technically precise, philosophically justified and sophisticated. Córdoba's continued access to and active study of such texts ensures the transmission of not merely technical techniques but complete frameworks of philosophical understanding. Knowledge transmission includes philosophy.
