Ray (Rhages)

Persia

c. 1096 CE, Rayy: The Emerald Tablet (Tabula Smaragdina), an ancient Hermetic text reinterpreted within Islamic alchemical frameworks, continues to guide Rayy's investigation of material transformation. This enigmatic text encodes the principles governing Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination, which Rayy's alchemists interpret as operations expressing cosmic law. The Tabula Smaragdina's influence demonstrates how Islamic alchemy synthesizes practical operative knowledge with cosmic philosophy. The continued circulation of this text in Rayy ensures the preservation of alchemical theory linking laboratory practice to universal principles.
