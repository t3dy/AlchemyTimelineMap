Scholarly Context

Michela Pereira demonstrated the high medieval period saw intensive assimilation of Arabic alchemical texts into Latin learning in *The Alchemical Corpus Attributed to Raymond Lull* (2007) ch.1.

c. 1050 CE, Paris: The manuscript Ms. Fr. 640 (Anonymous Technical Manuscript), a technical compendium on alchemical operations, begins to circulate in Western European scholarly networks. This text provides detailed descriptions of Distillation apparatus and methodology alongside theoretical justifications for Operational Chemistry. The circulation of such technical documents, translated from Arabic sources, marks the opening of systematic access to Islamic alchemical knowledge in the Latin West and catalyzes the emergence of medieval European alchemy.
