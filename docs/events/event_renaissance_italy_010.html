c. 1380 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Florence

Italy

c. 1380, Florence: The Florentine Neoplatonist revival intensifies as humanists systematically acquire and study Greek philosophical texts alongside alchemical commentaries, integrating Quintessence and Operational Chemistry into a unified vision of cosmic harmony. The recovery of the complete Corpus Hermeticum from Byzantine libraries provides intellectual ammunition for Renaissance thinkers seeking to reconcile alchemy with Platonic emanationism and Christian theology. This convergence establishes Florence as the epicenter of the philosophical hermetic tradition, contrasting sharply with Venice's pragmatic merchant-driven alchemical culture.

Concepts

  • Quintessence
  • Operational Chemistry
