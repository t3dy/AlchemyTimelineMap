c. 1650 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

London

England

c. 1650, London: The Protectorate and early Restoration period sees intense competition between alchemical and mechanical philosophies in English natural philosophical discourse. Advocates for Operational Chemistry and natural-philosophy increasingly adopt the rhetoric of mechanical causation while maintaining interest in material transmutation. This hybridization of alchemical and mechanical worldviews represents a critical moment in the eventual transformation of alchemy into chemistry.
