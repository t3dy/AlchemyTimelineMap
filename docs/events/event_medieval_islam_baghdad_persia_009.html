c. 808 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Ray (Rhages)

Persia

c. 808 CE, Rayy: The translator Gerard of Cremona symbolizes the future bridge between Islamic and Latin scholarship. In Rayy, alchemical knowledge reaches its zenith, with Al-Razi (Rhazes) actively investigating the Emerald Tablet (Tabula Smaragdina), a text encoding ancient Hermetic wisdom in alchemical language. The techniques of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination, now systematized and refined across Persian centers, will require translation into Latin and European vernaculars. Gerard's eventual role in transmitting Arabic learning signals the continuity of Islamic alchemy into medieval European scholarship.
