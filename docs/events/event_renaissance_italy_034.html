c. 1572 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Florence

Italy

c. 1572, Florence: The Florentine polymath Ulisse Aldrovandi completes his encyclopedic compilation of natural history and natural magic, incorporating extensive discussions of alchemical Transmutation, Operational Chemistry, and the properties of Quintessence. This monumental synthesis treats alchemy as an integral component of natural philosophy rather than as a marginal or disreputable pursuit, establishing its legitimacy within the emerging scientific worldview of the Renaissance.

Concepts

  • Transmutation
  • Quintessence
  • Operational Chemistry
