Scholarly Context

Deborah Harkness demonstrated that early modern London had active networks of alchemists and chemists pursuing practical experimentation in *The Jewel House* (2007) ch.7.

c. 1620, London: Under James I, alchemical research continues under royal patronage, though increasingly constrained by skepticism regarding transmutational claims. English natural philosophers distinguish more sharply between the verified techniques of Operational Chemistry (distillation, crystallization, dissolution) and the unverified claims of base metal transmutation into gold. This differentiation presages the eventual separation of chemistry from alchemy in the seventeenth century.
