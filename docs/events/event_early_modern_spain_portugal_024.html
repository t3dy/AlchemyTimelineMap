Madrid

Iberia

c. 1644 CE, Madrid: The Corpus Hermeticum, the philosophical cornerstone of Renaissance alchemy, continues to circulate in Madrid's scholarly circles, now read within frameworks of critical philology. Scholars debate the text's historical authorship and chronology while simultaneously defending Transmutation as natural philosophical enterprise. Madrid's alchemists increasingly distinguish between the metaphysical theology of Hermeticism and the practical empiricism of Operational Chemistry—a distinction that would eventually facilitate alchemy's transformation into chemistry. The critical reading of canonical alchemical texts marks the beginning of alchemy's reflexive self-examination.
