c. 720 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Constantinople

Byzantium

c. 720 CE, Toledo: The scholar Gerard of Cremona travels to Muslim Spain to acquire knowledge of Arabic natural philosophy and alchemical texts, beginning his lifelong project of translation. Gerard's methodical approach to rendering Arabic technical terminology into Latin prose will establish the linguistic foundations for transmitting Operational Chemistry and Distillation methodology to the Western scholarly community. His translation activities mark the opening of a critical conduit through which Islamic alchemy enters the Latin intellectual world.
