Scholarly Context

William Newman demonstrated calcination was understood as the reduction of metals toward prima materia in preparation for reconstitution in a purer form in *The Summa Perfectionis* (1991) pp.110-135.

c. 988 CE, Baghdad: The Corpus Hermeticum, a foundational Hermetic philosophical text incorporating alchemical allegory, continues to circulate and influence Baghdad's alchemical interpretation. The Hermetic framework within which Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination are understood emphasizes the cosmological and spiritual dimensions of material transformation. Baghdad's alchemists integrate this Hermetic perspective with the empirical rigor of Jabir ibn Hayyan and Al-Razi (Rhazes), creating a synthesis of operative chemistry and philosophical theology that profoundly shapes medieval European alchemy.
