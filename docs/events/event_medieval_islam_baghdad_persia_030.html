c. 1060 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Baghdad

Iraq

c. 1060 CE, Baghdad: The alchemical treatise De Sex Rerum Principiis (On the Six Principles of Things) continues to influence Baghdad's natural philosophy, offering a sophisticated theoretical framework for understanding matter and material transformation. This work, building upon Al-Razi (Rhazes)'s original composition, presents Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination as expressions of fundamental principles governing all material existence. Baghdad's philosophers and alchemists engage with this theoretical sophistication, integrating operative chemistry with comprehensive natural philosophy.
