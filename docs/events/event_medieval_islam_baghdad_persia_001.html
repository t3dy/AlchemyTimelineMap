Scholarly Context

Lawrence Principe showed al-Razi's Kitab al-Asrar provided the most systematic practical laboratory manual in Arabic alchemy in *The Secrets of Alchemy* (2013) pp.35-40.

c. 712 CE, Rayy: The physician and alchemist Al-Razi (Rhazes) establishes his medical and alchemical laboratory in Rayy (Rhages), then a major intellectual center of the Persian plateau. Al-Razi pioneers systematic experimentation with Distillation and Sublimation to isolate and test mineral essences, refining earlier techniques through quantitative observation. His rigorous approach to Calcination of mineral compounds marks a shift toward empirically grounded chemistry. Al-Razi's work demonstrates that alchemical operations yield reproducible, measurable results, earning him renown across the Islamic world and eventually reaching medieval European scholars.
