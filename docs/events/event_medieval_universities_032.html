Scholarly Context

Peter Adamson established continuities between Islamic and medieval Latin operational chemistry through careful analysis of apparatus descriptions in *The Arabic Aristotle* (2017) ch.8.

c. 1584 CE, Bologna: Bologna's medical faculty continues to maintain its tradition of integrating Operational Chemistry into professional medical training, with students studying the practical techniques of pharmaceutical preparation that have been refined over centuries of university instruction. By the late sixteenth century, the integration of alchemical operations into medical education at Bologna is so thoroughly established that it constitutes a standard feature of medical training rather than a novel or controversial subject. Bologna's sustained commitment to practical alchemical training ensures that the university remains relevant to medical professionals seeking knowledge of pharmaceutical preparation.
