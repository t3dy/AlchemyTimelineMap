c. 1388 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Venice

Italy

c. 1388, Venice: The Venetian state library acquires a comprehensive collection of Arabic alchemical manuscripts from a Cairo merchant, including texts on mineral Operational Chemistry and metal refinement. This acquisition cements Venice's position as the Mediterranean gateway for unmediated access to the most advanced Islamic alchemical knowledge available in the 14th century. The deposit of these manuscripts in a public institutional setting rather than a private collection marks a significant shift toward the democratization of alchemical learning in the Republic.

Concepts

  • Transmutation
