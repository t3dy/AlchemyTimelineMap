c. 1662 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Paris

France

c. 1662, Paris: The medieval and Islamic alchemical masterpiece Kitāb al-Ḥāsib, though increasingly treated as historical document rather than practical guide, continues to inform French chemical research among scholars reconstructing alchemy's scientific and technical achievements. Late seventeenth-century readers of Jabir ibn Hayyan attempt to understand what his systematic chemical-practice actually accomplished, separating legitimate chemistry from metaphysical speculation. The critical historical study of Islamic alchemy in late seventeenth-century Paris contributed to recognition of alchemy's genuine technical accomplishments.
