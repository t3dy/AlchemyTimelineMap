c. 1620 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Prague

Bohemia

c. 1620, Prague: The early years of the Thirty Years War disrupt but do not entirely extinguish Prague's alchemical research tradition, as some practitioners continue laboratory work despite military and political upheaval. Prague's chemical practitioners adapted to wartime conditions while maintaining core laboratory operations. The preservation of alchemical knowledge through the chaos of religious warfare represents a significant instance of transmission continuity under extreme historical stress.
