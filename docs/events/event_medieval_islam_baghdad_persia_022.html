c. 964 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Baghdad

Iraq

c. 964 CE, Baghdad: The institutionalization of alchemical learning in Baghdad's schools ensures the preservation and continued refinement of chemical operations. Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination remain central to the curriculum, alongside theoretical instruction in natural philosophy and cosmology. Baghdad's alchemical community maintains the rigorous standards of precision and reproducibility established by Al-Razi (Rhazes) and encoded in the works of Jabir ibn Hayyan. The disciplinary maturity achieved in Baghdad becomes the model that European alchemists will eventually emulate.

Concepts

  • Distillation
  • Sublimation
  • Calcination
