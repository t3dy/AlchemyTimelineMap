Madrid

Iberia

c. 1572 CE, Madrid: The Picatrix (Ghāyat al-Hakīm), a medieval astrological-magical text, circulates among Spanish scholars seeking to understand the correlation between celestial movements and terrestrial transformation. The Picatrix's elaborate system of sympathetic magic provides philosophical justification for Operational Chemistry understood as participation in cosmic forces. Spanish scholars debate whether Transmutation operates through natural causation or through astrological influence, creating sophisticated theoretical frameworks that integrate Aristotelian physics, Neoplatonic philosophy, and practical alchemy. Madrid becomes a locus of syncretic alchemical theory, where multiple intellectual traditions converge in attempts to legitimate alchemy as natural magic grounded in rational principles.
