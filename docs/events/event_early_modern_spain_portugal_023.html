Scholarly Context

Deborah Harkness demonstrated that Lisbon was a crucial hub for the circulation of alchemical knowledge through maritime trade and printing in early modern Europe in *The Jewel House* (2007) ch.6.

c. 1638 CE, Lisbon: Portuguese alchemists and physicians, informed by decades of accumulated technical knowledge and theoretical sophistication, refine their understanding of Operational Chemistry and its relationship to medical practice. Large-scale production of mineral acids, alkalis, and quintessences sustains an economically significant chemical industry. The routinization of Transmutation operations—repeated thousands of times with consistent results—grounds alchemical claims in observable regularity rather than mystical hope. Lisbon's merchant and scholarly communities have fully integrated alchemical-chemical practice into urban commercial and professional life.
