c. 780 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Constantinople

Byzantium

c. 780 CE, Baghdad: Scholars in the Abbasid court study and copy the De Sex Rerum Principiis (On the Six Principles of Things), a foundational treatise on Operational Chemistry and the material composition of bodies. The text's systematic treatment of elemental transmutation and the operations by which substances undergo material transformation exemplifies the intellectual ambitions of early medieval Islamic alchemy. The preservation and circulation of this work through Baghdad's scholarly networks ensures its eventual transmission to the Latin West.
