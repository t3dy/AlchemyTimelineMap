Bruges

Low Countries

c. 1504, Bruges: Bruges entered the 16th century with an established tradition of alchemical scholarship and practice, though commercial and political dominance increasingly accrued to Antwerp. Practitioners in Bruges continued systematic engagement with Distillation and chemical-experimentation informed by centuries of textual and practical transmission. The city's role as a center for artistic and intellectual refinement meant that alchemy retained prestige as both theoretical and practical knowledge.
