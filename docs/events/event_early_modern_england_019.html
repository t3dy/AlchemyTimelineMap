c. 1614 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

London

England

c. 1614, London: English alchemists pursue increasingly sophisticated experiments in the isolation and concentration of mineral essences, employing improved distillation apparatus and crystallization techniques. The mastery of Operational Chemistry has reached such sophistication that English practitioners now exchange technical innovations with Continental counterparts as peers rather than students. This represents the achievement of genuine English independence in alchemical practice and theory.
