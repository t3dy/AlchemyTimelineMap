Scholarly Context

Mauricio Sánchez Martínez showed that Madrid's royal court patronage supported alchemical research in early modern Spain in *The Construction of an Identity* (2005).

c. 1548 CE, Madrid: Spanish alchemists and physicians engaged in producing mineral acids and alkalis through distillation establish the technical foundations of early modern pharmaceutical chemistry. Practitioners of Operational Chemistry work in university-affiliated laboratories and private workshops, applying alchemical methodology to the systematic production of medicinal compounds. The integration of Transmutation theory into medical practice creates a discourse where the transformation of matter becomes inseparable from healing. Madrid's position as a royal capital ensures patronage and prestige for these alchemical-medical practitioners, establishing alchemy as an indispensable component of early modern learned medicine and natural philosophy.
