Scholarly Context

Brian Copenhaver showed that Florentine humanists and scholars integrated Hermetic and alchemical texts into the broader project of recovering ancient wisdom in *Hermetica* (1992) ch.3.

c. 1476, Florence: The first printed edition of a significant alchemical text is produced in Florence, representing a revolutionary shift from manuscript circulation to mass production and distribution of Transmutation theory. The printing press, recently arrived in Italy, accelerates the democratization of alchemical knowledge, allowing far greater numbers of scholars, apothecaries, and artisans to access sophisticated texts on Operational Chemistry. This development transforms Florence's intellectual advantage into market dominance in alchemical publishing.
