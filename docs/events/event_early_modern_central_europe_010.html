c. 1560 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Prague

Bohemia

c. 1560, Prague: Prague's alchemical research tradition continues under imperial patronage, with laboratory practitioners pursuing systematic investigations into transmutation and the production of medicinal distillates from mineral and plant sources. The Prague tradition of the 1560s consolidated the intellectual legacy of Paracelsus and Agrippa into an institutional framework for chemical research. This decade witnessed Prague's establishment as the premier center of alchemical learning north of Italy, attracting scholars from across Europe.
