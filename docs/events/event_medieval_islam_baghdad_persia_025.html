Ray (Rhages)

Persia

Scholarly Context

Lawrence Principe showed sublimation was used to purify and concentrate volatile components in both pharmaceutical preparation and transmutational operations in *The Secrets of Alchemy* (2013) pp.18-22.

c. 1000 CE, Rayy: The continuation of systematic alchemical practice in Rayy at the millennium demonstrates the institutionalization of Islamic chemistry. Practitioners and philosophers maintain the traditions of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination, refining apparatus and refining theoretical understanding. Rayy's role as a major center of alchemical learning persists despite broader political changes in the Islamic world. The preservation of alchemical texts and the continuity of experimental practice ensure that the knowledge accumulated over three centuries remains accessible to future scholars, including eventual medieval European translators.
