c. 1120 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Ray (Rhages)

Persia

c. 1120 CE, Rayy: The continuation of alchemical investigation in Rayy during the early twelfth century demonstrates the discipline's deep entrenchment in Islamic natural philosophy. Practitioners maintain laboratories equipped with sophisticated apparatus for Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination. Rayy's alchemical schools offer comprehensive instruction in operative technique and theoretical understanding. The legacy of Al-Razi (Rhazes) and Jabir ibn Hayyan remains vital, guiding investigation into the nature of matter and the principles governing material transformation.

Concepts

  • Distillation
  • Sublimation
  • Calcination
