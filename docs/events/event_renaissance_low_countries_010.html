c. 1480 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Antwerp

Low Countries

c. 1480, Antwerp: Antwerp's merchants and scholars engaged actively with alchemical literature and practice as the 15th century closed. The systematic study of Distillation and chemical-experimentation had become integrated into the city's broader commercial activities, particularly in the preparation of pharmaceuticals and dyes. This moment captures Antwerp on the threshold of becoming the single most important center of practical alchemy in northern Europe during the coming centuries.
