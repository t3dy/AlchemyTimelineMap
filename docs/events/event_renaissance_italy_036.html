c. 1588 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Florence

Italy

c. 1588, Florence: The Grand Ducal laboratory in Florence achieves significant technical innovations in distillation apparatus design and thermal management, advancing the practical state of Operational Chemistry beyond what was possible in earlier eras. These improvements, developed through systematic experimentation under princely patronage, allow practitioners to achieve previously unattainable degrees of purity and precision in Quintessence preparation and metal refinement.
