Ray (Rhages)

Persia

Scholarly Context

Lawrence Principe showed al-Razi's Kitab al-Asrar provided the most systematic practical laboratory manual in Arabic alchemy in *The Secrets of Alchemy* (2013) pp.35-40.

c. 832 CE, Rayy: The alchemist Al-Razi (Rhazes) continues his experimental investigations into the nature of matter and the limits of transmutation. In Rayy, al-Razi's sophisticated laboratory practice refines the techniques of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination, establishing standards for chemical purity and measurement. His empirical rigor and willingness to challenge inherited assumptions transform alchemy from a collection of craft secrets into a systematic natural philosophy. Al-Razi's influence extends across the Islamic world and eventually reaches medieval Europe through translated excerpts and paraphrases.
