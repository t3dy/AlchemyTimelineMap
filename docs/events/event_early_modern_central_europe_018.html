Scholarly Context

Hereward Tilton demonstrated Prague under Rudolf II became a major center for Renaissance hermetic and alchemical learning in *The Quest for the Phoenix* (2003) ch.6.

c. 1608, Prague: The Summa Perfectionis (Sum of Perfection), the foundational medieval Arabic text attributed to Jabir ibn Hayyan on systematic distillation and the production of strong acids and metal solvents, continues to guide Prague's laboratory practitioners in their pursuit of transmutation and chemical innovation. The Summa's practical instructions remained the most authoritative guide to laboratory operations across Central European alchemical research. Prague's practitioners maintained the unbroken transmission of Jabirian operational knowledge from medieval Islam to early modern Europe.
