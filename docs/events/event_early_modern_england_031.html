c. 1686 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

London

England

c. 1686, London: Newton's Principia Mathematica establishes mathematical mechanics as the authoritative framework for natural philosophy, rendering alchemical explanation obsolete even as the techniques of Operational Chemistry persist. The transmutational aspirations that animated English alchemy for two centuries are now confined to private notebooks and hidden experiments. The public discourse of English natural philosophy has decisively rejected alchemy while retaining its technical methods.
