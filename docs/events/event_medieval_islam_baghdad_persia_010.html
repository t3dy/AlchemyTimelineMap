c. 820 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Baghdad

Iraq

c. 820 CE, Baghdad: Jabir ibn Hayyan reaches the height of his intellectual authority, his vast corpus of alchemical and chemical works now firmly established as the standard reference in Baghdad and throughout the Islamic world. Jabir's systematization of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination techniques, coupled with his insistence on precise measurement and reproducibility, establishes chemistry as a rigorous discipline. His works, eventually translated into Latin as the Summa Perfectionis and other compilations, will dominate European alchemy for centuries, making Jabir the single most influential figure in the Western alchemical tradition.
