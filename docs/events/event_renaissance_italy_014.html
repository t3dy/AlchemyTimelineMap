Scholarly Context

Brian Copenhaver showed that Florentine humanists and scholars integrated Hermetic and alchemical texts into the broader project of recovering ancient wisdom in *Hermetica* (1992) ch.3.

c. 1412, Florence: The Florentine merchant Vespasiano da Bisticci, a major book dealer, begins actively acquiring alchemical texts and manuscripts on Transmutation theory for the city's bibliophiles and scholars. His commercial enterprise in rare books becomes an instrument for intellectual agenda-setting, ensuring that Florentine humanists have first access to newly recovered Greek hermetic and alchemical texts from Constantinople. Through Bisticci's network, Florence consolidates its identity as the primary center of alchemical learning in the Western world.
