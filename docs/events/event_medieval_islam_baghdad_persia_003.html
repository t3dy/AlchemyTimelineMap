c. 736 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Ray (Rhages)

Persia

c. 736 CE, Rayy: The Kitāb al-Ḥāsib, attributed to Jabir ibn Hayyan, circulates in Rayy and among Persian natural philosophers exploring operative chemistry. This foundational text systematizes techniques of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination, presenting them as reproducible procedures grounded in rational investigation. The Kitāb al-Ḥāsib becomes the standard reference for alchemists across Persia and Baghdad, establishing Jabir as the preeminent authority on chemical operations. Its circulation marks the transition of alchemy from oral tradition to canonized textual knowledge.
