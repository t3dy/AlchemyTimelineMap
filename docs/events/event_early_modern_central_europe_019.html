Scholarly Context

Michael McVaugh demonstrated that iatrochemical transmutation goals were pursued in medieval pharmaceutical contexts in *The Rational Surgery of the Middle Ages* (2006) ch.2.

c. 1614, Vienna: Vienna's alchemical research continues during a period of increasing religious and political tension that will soon overwhelm the region. Vienna's laboratories maintained commitment to transmutation research and metallurgical innovation. This period represents the final flourishing of imperial alchemical patronage before the catastrophe of the Thirty Years War disrupted European learning.
