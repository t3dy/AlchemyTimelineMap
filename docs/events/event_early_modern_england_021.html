c. 1626 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

London

England

c. 1626, London: English scholars circulate and study the medieval alchemical compilation Liber XXIV Philosophorum (Book of Twenty-Four Philosophers), seeking to extract metaphysical principles that justify continued Operational Chemistry research even as transmutational claims face increasing skepticism. The Liber's assertion of divine unity underlying material multiplicity resonates with English Christian natural philosophers seeking a theological framework for their chemical investigations. This text becomes influential in reconciling alchemy with Anglican theology.
