c. 1168 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Ray (Rhages)

Persia

c. 1168 CE, Rayy: The anonymous technical manuscript Ms. Fr. 640 (Anonymous Technical Manuscript) exemplifies the sophisticated state of alchemical documentation in Rayy during the twelfth century. This text records detailed procedures for Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination, reflecting generations of refined practice and theoretical understanding. The manuscript's careful attention to apparatus design, material selection, and operational precision demonstrates the high level of chemical expertise maintained in Rayy. Such technical documentation preserves the practical knowledge of Islamic alchemy for transmission and eventual adoption by medieval European scholars.
