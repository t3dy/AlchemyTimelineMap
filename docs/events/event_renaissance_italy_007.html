c. 1356 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Venice

Italy

c. 1356, Venice: The Venetian apothecaries' guild formally incorporates instruction in distillation, sublimation, and calcination of pharmaceutical materials into its apprenticeship curriculum, elevating Operational Chemistry to a regulated craft standard. This institutional codification ensures systematic transmission of practical alchemical knowledge to successive generations of apothecaries and physicians. The guild's formal recognition of alchemical operations as legitimate professional training marks a watershed moment when Venice institutionalizes the transition from oral tradition to written, examined expertise.
