Green Lion

Type: TREATISE | Language: None | Composed: ca. 1500

The Green Lion is an influential English alchemical text existing in multiple manuscript versions, demonstrating how texts were adapted and modified as they circulated among practitioners. Named after the alchemical symbol of the Green Lion (vitriol), a key material in operations, the text exemplifies the integration of practical chemistry with symbolic understanding.

Different manuscript variants of Green Lion reveal the textual genealogy of English alchemical texts: evidence of copying, modification, annotation, and reinterpretation by successive readers. The text's circulation in both English and Continental sources demonstrates how English alchemical materials participated in broader European knowledge networks while maintaining distinctive characteristics.
