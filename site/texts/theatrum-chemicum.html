Theatrum Chemicum

Type: TREATISE | Language: None | Composed: c. 1602

The Theatrum Chemicum (Chemical Theater) is a massive printed anthology of alchemical texts compiled and published in early seventeenth-century Strasbourg. The collection assembles Latin translations and original works covering the full historical range of alchemical literature, from texts attributed to Zosimos through contemporary Renaissance authors. The Theatrum Chemicum represents the culmination of printed alchemical publishing in Renaissance and early modern Europe, gathering in one place texts that had previously circulated in manuscript form. Its publication had enormous influence, making comprehensive alchemical literature available to a broad audience of scholars, physicians, and practitioners. The Theatrum Chemicum served as the standard reference for alchemical texts through the eighteenth century and remains an essential primary source for understanding how early modern scholars understood the alchemical tradition.
