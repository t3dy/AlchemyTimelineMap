Henry Cornelius Agrippa

Role: PHILOSOPHER | Era: EARLY_MODERN

1486–1535, German polymath and occultist. Authored De Occulta Philosophia, synthesizing Hermetic, Kabbalistic, and alchemical traditions into systematic philosophy of natural magic.
