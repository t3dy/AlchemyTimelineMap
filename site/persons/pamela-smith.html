Pamela H. Smith

Role: SCHOLAR | Era: MODERN

Seth Low Professor of History at Columbia University. Founding Director of the Center for Science and Society. Pioneer of artisanal epistemology framework and founder of the Making and Knowing Project (2014). Theorizes how craft knowledge and embodied practice were foundational to the Scientific Revolution.
