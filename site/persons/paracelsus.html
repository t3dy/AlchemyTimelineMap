Paracelsus (Theophrastus Bombastus von Hohenheim)

Role: PHYSICIAN | Era: EARLY_MODERN

1493–1541, Swiss physician and alchemist. Pioneered medical alchemy focused on pharmaceutical preparation and healing rather than transmutation. His framework influenced Renaissance and early modern alchemical practice.
