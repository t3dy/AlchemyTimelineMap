c. 1380 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Prague

Bohemia

c. 1380 CE, Prague: Scholars at the University of Prague encounter and study the Ms. Fr. 640 (Anonymous Technical Manuscript), an anonymous technical manuscript circulating in Northern Europe that provides practical instructions for distillation, acid production, and other alchemical operations alongside theoretical justifications grounded in natural philosophy. The manuscript's focus on practical technique combined with natural philosophical explanation appeals to Prague's educational culture, which values both empirical investigation and theoretical understanding. The circulation of practical technical manuscripts like Ms. Fr. 640 within the Praguian curriculum reflects the university's integration of both theoretical and practical knowledge in its teaching of Operational Chemistry.
