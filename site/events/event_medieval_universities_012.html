Scholarly Context

Paul Kristeller showed Bologna was a major center for medical and natural philosophical study, including alchemical topics, in *Renaissance Thought and its Sources* (1979).

c. 1344 CE, Bologna: Scholars at Bologna circulate and study the Picatrix (Ghāyat al-Hakīm) (Ghāyat al-Hakīm), an Islamic encyclopedic work on natural magic, astrology, and the properties of materials, which provides an alternative framework for understanding Operational Chemistry as part of a broader philosophy of cosmic correspondences and the influence of celestial bodies on terrestrial substances. The Picatrix's sophisticated treatment of how practitioners can manipulate material properties through understanding celestial influences attracts interest from natural philosophers at Bologna seeking to integrate alchemical knowledge with astrological theory. This circulation of the Picatrix within the Bolognese curriculum broadens the intellectual context in which alchemical operations are understood.
