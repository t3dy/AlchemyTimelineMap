c. 1404 CE Medieval (7th–15th c.)

Venice

Italy

c. 1404, Venice: The Venetian humanist Francesco Barbaro, a prominent figure in the city's intellectual revival, begins collecting Greek and Latin texts on natural philosophy and alchemy, establishing a private library that becomes a magnet for scholars seeking primary sources on Quintessence and Operational Chemistry. His efforts to systematize knowledge of alchemical texts parallel the Florentine humanist project, but with Venice's characteristic merchant-class pragmatism and direct connection to Byzantine learning.

Concepts

  • Transmutation
  • Quintessence
  • Operational Chemistry
