Scholarly Context

Andrew Pettegree showed that print culture was central to the dissemination of alchemical texts in early modern Europe in *The Book in the Renaissance* (2010) ch.4.

c. 1628, Venice: The Venetian printshops produce multiple new editions and translations of canonical alchemical texts, including works on Transmutation, distillation techniques, and Quintessence preparation, ensuring that alchemical knowledge continues to circulate widely despite the Church's increasing skepticism toward transmutational claims. The Republic's continued investment in alchemical publishing testifies to the persistent demand for practical and theoretical guidance in Operational Chemistry.
