Scholarly Context

Lawrence Principe showed Roger Bacon distinguished legitimate experimental alchemy from fraudulent gold-making, defending transmutation's theoretical possibility in *The Secrets of Alchemy* (2013) pp.57-63.

c. 916 CE, Baghdad: The scholar Roger Bacon symbolizes the future reception of Baghdad's alchemical legacy in medieval Europe. By this date, the Summa Perfectionis (Sum of Perfection), attributed to Jabir ibn Hayyan, has achieved textual canonical status in Baghdad and throughout Islamic scholarly centers. Bacon's eventual transmission and development of this work in Oxford will make it the most authoritative alchemical reference in medieval Europe. The operative chemistry of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination established in Baghdad becomes the foundation for European alchemy through Bacon's learned authority.
