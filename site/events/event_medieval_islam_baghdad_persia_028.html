Scholarly Context

Lawrence Principe showed sublimation was used to purify and concentrate volatile components in both pharmaceutical preparation and transmutational operations in *The Secrets of Alchemy* (2013) pp.18-22.

c. 1036 CE, Baghdad: Baghdad's scholarly institutions maintain the disciplinary tradition of alchemical investigation established centuries earlier. Though the Abbasid caliphate has fragmented politically, Baghdad's intellectual prestige ensures continued support for advanced studies in natural philosophy. The operative techniques of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination, now refined through centuries of practice, represent the highest achievement in material chemistry. Baghdad's alchemists maintain rigorous laboratories and continue to advance both theoretical understanding and practical expertise.
