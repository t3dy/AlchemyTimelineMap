Scholarly Context

Danielle Jacquart and Francoise Micheau showed transmutation aspirations structured Arabic alchemical practice and medicine in *The Natural History of the Universe in Arabic Sources* (2008) ch.5.

c. 1644, Paris: The Corpus Hermeticum, once celebrated as a repository of ancient wisdom concerning Transmutation and natural magic, faces withering criticism from early modern scholars who recognize it as a late antique composition rather than ancient Egyptian philosophy. Despite this erudite debunking, Parisian alchemists and occultists continue to mine the Corpus for support of Transmutation and magical worldviews. The conflict between historical-critical scholarship and alchemical interpretation exemplifies larger tensions between emerging scholarly rigor and older knowledge traditions. The historical criticism of Corpus Hermeticum in mid-seventeenth-century Paris challenged alchemy's foundational authorities.
