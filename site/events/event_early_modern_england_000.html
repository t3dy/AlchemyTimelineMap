Scholarly Context

Peter Adamson established continuities between Islamic and medieval Latin operational chemistry through careful analysis of apparatus descriptions in *The Arabic Aristotle* (2017) ch.8.

c. 1500, London: As the Tudor dynasty consolidates power, English scholars and courtiers begin acquiring Continental alchemical texts, including translations and commentaries on the Corpus Hermeticum, which had circulated widely in Renaissance Italy. This influx represents the first systematic introduction of Hermetic natural philosophy into the English court, establishing a tradition of royal patronage for alchemy that would persist through the sixteenth century. The Corpus Hermeticum teachings on divine emanation and cosmic sympathy profoundly influenced how English intellectuals understood the hidden connections between celestial and terrestrial substances.
