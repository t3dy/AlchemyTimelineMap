Antwerp

Low Countries

c. 1560, Antwerp: The mid-16th century confirmed Antwerp's unprecedented dominance in European alchemy, with the city hosting not only practitioners but also a rapidly expanding literature on practical and theoretical chemistry. The systematic engagement with Distillation and chemical-experimentation had become integrated into the city's educational structures, with knowledge transmitted through formal apprenticeships and informal patronage relationships. Antwerp had become the effective capital of alchemical Europe.
