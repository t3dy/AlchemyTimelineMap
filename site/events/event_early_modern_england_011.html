c. 1566 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

London

England

c. 1566, London: Elizabeth I establishes formal patronage of alchemical research aimed at testing transmutational claims and improving English mineral extraction. Court-sponsored experiments in alchemy attract Continental experts and English natural philosophers seeking royal favor and financial support. This institutionalization of alchemical practice within royal prerogative distinguishes England from other European courts and establishes alchemy as an affair of state rather than merely private inquiry.
