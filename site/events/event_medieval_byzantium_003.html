Constantinople

Byzantium

c. 690 CE, Oxford: The natural philosopher Roger Bacon studies early alchemical texts including the Kitāb al-Ḥāsib, attributed to Jabir ibn Hayyan, seeking to understand the relationship between Distillation and broader Operational Chemistry. Bacon's engagement with Arabic alchemical sources reflects the growing penetration of Islamic scientific knowledge into Western universities. His emphasis on experimental verification and mathematical precision in describing chemical operations anticipates the methodological rigor that will characterize early modern alchemy.
