Ray (Rhages)

Persia

Scholarly Context

William Newman traced the Jabirian corpus as the foundational Arabic synthesis establishing the sulphur-mercury theory as the dominant framework for metallic composition in *The Summa Perfectionis* (1991).

c. 880 CE, Rayy: The Ms. Fr. 640 (Anonymous Technical Manuscript), an anonymous technical manuscript, represents the continued development of alchemical practice in Rayy, building upon the foundations established by Jabir ibn Hayyan and Al-Razi (Rhazes). This manuscript documents advanced techniques of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination refined through generations of experimental practice. The methodological precision and theoretical sophistication evident in such texts demonstrate the maturity of Islamic chemistry by the ninth century. Such documents will eventually circulate in Europe, influencing alchemists and natural philosophers.
