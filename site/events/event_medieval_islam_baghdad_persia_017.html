Scholarly Context

Peter Adamson established al-Kindi systematized the Neoplatonic framework for celestial influence on terrestrial substances in *Al-Kindi* (2007) ch.4.

c. 904 CE, Rayy: The philosopher Al-Kindi (al-Kindy), though deceased by this date, exerts posthumous influence on Rayy's intellectual culture through his preserved writings on natural philosophy and alchemy. Al-Kindi's critical framework for evaluating alchemical claims—demanding both rational coherence and empirical evidence—becomes institutionalized in advanced studies at Rayy. His skepticism regarding transmutation, coupled with his acknowledgment of the validity of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination, establishes a model for distinguishing legitimate chemical practice from speculative metaphysics.
