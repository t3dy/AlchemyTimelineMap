Scholarly Context

Peter Adamson established continuities between Islamic and medieval Latin operational chemistry through careful analysis of apparatus descriptions in *The Arabic Aristotle* (2017) ch.8.

c. 1596 CE, Paris: Scholars at Paris encounter and study renewed editions of the Emerald Tablet (Tabula Smaragdina), the foundational Hermetic text whose circulation within the Parisian curriculum since the fourteenth century has provided philosophical warrant for viewing alchemical operations as expressions of universal natural law. By the late sixteenth century, the Emerald Tablet has become a canonical text for European natural philosophers and alchemists seeking to integrate Hermetic philosophy with empirical investigation of matter. Paris's sustained engagement with Hermetic texts ensures that the university remains at the forefront of European Hermetic scholarship into the early modern period.
