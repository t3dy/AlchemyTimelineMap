c. 1428 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Florence

Italy

c. 1428, Florence: The Florentine philosopher and theologian Giovanni Pico della Mirandola (though not yet prominent) inherits a library rich in hermetic and alchemical texts, laying the intellectual foundation for his later synthesis of Hebrew Kabbalah, Neoplatonic philosophy, and alchemical Transmutation theory. This generational transfer of knowledge within Florence's elite families ensures the continuity of sophisticated alchemical learning in the city's intellectual establishment.
