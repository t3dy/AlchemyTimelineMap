Scholarly Context

Paula Findlen demonstrated that Vienna's imperial court patronage supported alchemical research as part of the Kunstkammer tradition in *Possessing Nature* (1994) ch.6.

c. 1554, Vienna: The Emerald Tablet (Tabula Smaragdina), circulating in multiple Latin translations and commentaries, continues to provide the foundational philosophical principle for Vienna's alchemical researchers, who ground their laboratory work in Hermetic doctrine. The Emerald Tablet's aphorism "as above, so below" directed alchemical practice toward the investigation of correspondence between celestial operations and terrestrial laboratory transformations. The tablet's circulation in Vienna establishes the theoretical framework that justified transmutation as a legitimate application of natural philosophy.
