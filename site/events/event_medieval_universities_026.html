Scholarly Context

Allen Debus showed the early modern period witnessed the gradual transformation of alchemy into chymistry and chemical medicine in *The Chemical Philosophy* (1977) vol.1 ch.1.

c. 1512 CE, Oxford: Oxford's natural philosophy faculty continues to maintain its distinctive tradition of combining rigorous critical analysis with empirical investigation in Operational Chemistry. By the early sixteenth century, the university's long tradition of scholarly engagement with alchemical texts and operations has produced an intellectual culture capable of sophisticated evaluation of alchemical claims against the standards of Aristotelian natural philosophy and logical reasoning. Oxford's transition into the early modern period is marked by the consolidation of its medieval achievements in alchemical studies into a stable, authoritative scholarly tradition.
