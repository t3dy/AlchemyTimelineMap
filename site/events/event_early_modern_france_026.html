Scholarly Context

Danielle Jacquart and Francoise Micheau showed transmutation aspirations structured Arabic alchemical practice and medicine in *The Natural History of the Universe in Arabic Sources* (2008) ch.5.

c. 1656, Paris: The mid-seventeenth century sees consolidation of new chemical frameworks that preserve operational knowledge from alchemy while abandoning transmutation as an achievable goal. Parisian chemists, influenced by iatrochemistry and mechanical philosophy, focus on producing therapeutically valuable substances through chemical-practice, treating success in medicine as vindication enough without claiming ultimate Transmutation. This reframing allows former alchemists to maintain laboratory practice and intellectual respectability within the emerging scientific order. The emergence of therapeutic chemistry in mid-seventeenth-century Paris established the framework for modern pharmaceutical science.
