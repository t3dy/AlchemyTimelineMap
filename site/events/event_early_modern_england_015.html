Scholarly Context

Deborah Harkness demonstrated that early modern London had active networks of alchemists and chemists pursuing practical experimentation in *The Jewel House* (2007) ch.7.

c. 1590, London: The French alchemical manuscript Ms. Fr. 640 (Anonymous Technical Manuscript), containing detailed chemical procedures and operational instructions, circulates among English courtiers and is copied into English collections. This anonymous technical treatise's emphasis on verifiable chemical processes and reproducible results aligns perfectly with the emerging English approach to alchemy as an empirical rather than mystical discipline. The text becomes influential in shaping how Elizabethan alchemists systematize their operations.
