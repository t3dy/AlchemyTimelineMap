c. 1436 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Venice

Italy

c. 1436, Venice: The Venetian apothecary guild formally establishes a laboratory for standardized production of medicinal essences and distillates, incorporating the latest techniques of Operational Chemistry drawn from both Arabic and European sources. This institutional investment in pharmaceutical alchemy reflects Venice's growing integration of alchemical practice into medical and commercial enterprises, establishing public oversight of the quality and safety of alchemical products.
