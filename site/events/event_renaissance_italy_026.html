c. 1508 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Florence

Italy

c. 1508, Florence: The Florentine artist and natural philosopher Leonardo da Vinci engages seriously with alchemical theory and practice, recording detailed observations on distillation, mineral transformation, and the properties of Quintessence in his notebooks. Leonardo's synthesis of empirical observation, artistic imagination, and alchemical philosophy represents the full flowering of Renaissance integration of alchemy into natural investigation, treating Operational Chemistry as a valid mode of understanding the material world's hidden structures.

Concepts

  • Transmutation
  • Quintessence
  • Operational Chemistry
