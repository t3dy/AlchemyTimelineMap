Scholarly Context

Paula Findlen demonstrated that court patronage of alchemy was part of the broader Kunstkammer and natural history collecting tradition in *Possessing Nature* (1994) ch.7.

c. 1632, Prague: Prague's alchemical tradition, severely disrupted by the Thirty Years War, begins gradual recovery of laboratory practice and research during a period of tentative political stabilization. Prague's recovery of chemical research after military devastation represents the resilience of institutional commitment to alchemical knowledge. This recovery established the foundation for Prague's continued importance as a center of chemical research through the seventeenth century.
