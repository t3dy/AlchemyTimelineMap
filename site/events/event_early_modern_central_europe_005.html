c. 1530 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Vienna

Austria

c. 1530, Vienna: During this period of intense alchemical activity in Vienna under imperial patronage, alchemists and physicians pursue laboratory investigations into transmutation and the production of quintessences and medicinal spirits. The Vienna laboratory tradition of the 1530s represents a consolidation of transmission knowledge from medieval alchemy into systematic early modern practice. This era marks the establishment of Vienna as a major center of institutional patronage for alchemical research within the Holy Roman Empire, with consequences for the next two centuries of European chemical knowledge.
