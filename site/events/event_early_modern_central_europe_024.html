Prague

Bohemia

c. 1644, Prague: The Corpus Hermeticum, the foundational text of Hermetic philosophy and cosmology, continues to provide theoretical grounding for Prague's alchemical researchers as they work to reconcile laboratory operations with Neoplatonic philosophy and divine creative causation. The Corpus Hermeticum's sophisticated metaphysics directed Prague's alchemists toward cosmological understanding of transmutation. Prague's engagement with Hermetic texts sustained the philosophical ambitions of practical chemistry even after military devastation.
