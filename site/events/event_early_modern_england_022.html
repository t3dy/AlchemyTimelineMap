c. 1632 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

London

England

c. 1632, London: English alchemists face increasing institutional pressure from the newly established Royal Society's epistemological standards, which demand rigorous experimental verification and reject claims that cannot be universally replicated. The tradition of English alchemy, grounded in Hermetic metaphysics and transmutational aspiration, begins to fracture as Operational Chemistry is reframed as pure chemistry while alchemy is relegated to heterodoxy. This moment marks the beginning of alchemy's marginalization in English natural philosophy.
