c. 1638 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

London

England

c. 1638, London: Isaac Newton, though not yet publicly known as an alchemical researcher, begins his intensive study of alchemical texts and experiments in pursuit of understanding the hidden forces that govern matter. Newton's integration of Operational Chemistry, mathematical natural-philosophy, and hermetic theory will eventually define a new epoch in English natural science. His private alchemical work, kept hidden from the Royal Society, represents the apotheosis of English alchemical ambition before its eclipse.
