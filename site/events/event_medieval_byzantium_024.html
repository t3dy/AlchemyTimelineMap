Scholarly Context

Peter Adamson established continuities between Islamic and medieval Latin operational chemistry through careful analysis of apparatus descriptions in *The Arabic Aristotle* (2017) ch.8.

c. 1320 CE, Florence: The Corpus Hermeticum, recovered from Byzantine sources and circulated in Latin translation, profoundly influences European alchemical theory and practice. This foundational Hermetic text links Operational Chemistry to cosmic principles and divine knowledge, providing philosophical authority for the pursuit of transmutation and chemical transformation. The Corpus Hermeticum's integration into European alchemy establishes Hermetic philosophy as the dominant intellectual framework for Renaissance and early modern alchemical thought.
