Scholarly Context

Leah DeVun showed transmutation was pursued within mystical and theological frameworks by medieval and early modern alchemists in *Prophecy, Alchemy, and the End of Time* (2020) ch.4.

c. 1548, Venice: The Venetian apothecaries establish an independent scientific society dedicated to the systematic study of distillation, crystallization, and other Operational Chemistry techniques, meeting regularly to report observations and standardize procedures. This collegial association of practitioners represents an early institutional form of experimental natural philosophy, predating the formal scientific academies of the following century. The society's work demonstrates Venice's commitment to systematic, collective advancement of practical knowledge.
