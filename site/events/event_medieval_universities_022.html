Scholarly Context

Mordechai Feingold showed operational chemistry was integrated into university curricula and natural philosophy teaching in *The Decline and Rise of European Jewry* (1992) ch.5.

c. 1464 CE, Oxford: Oxford's scholars continue their tradition of rigorous critical engagement with Operational Chemistry and natural-philosophy, analyzing alchemical texts through the lens of Aristotelian logic and systematic natural philosophy. By the mid-fifteenth century, Oxford's intellectual culture has developed such sophistication in analyzing alchemical claims that the university attracts scholars seeking advanced training in the critical evaluation of alchemical texts and operations. Oxford's reputation for scholarly rigor applied to alchemy establishes a distinctive intellectual identity that will influence Renaissance natural philosophy.
