Scholarly Context

Peter Adamson established continuities between Islamic and medieval Latin operational chemistry through careful analysis of apparatus descriptions in *The Arabic Aristotle* (2017) ch.8.

c. 1584, London: Dr. John Dee's vast alchemical library and experimental apparatus make London the acknowledged center of English hermetic learning. Dee's work in natural-philosophy and Operational Chemistry, grounded in both practical experimentation and Neoplatonic theory, establishes a model of alchemy as rigorous natural inquiry rather than mystical speculation. His patronage networks and manuscript collections transform London into the intellectual capital of English Hermetic philosophy.
