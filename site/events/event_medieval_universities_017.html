Scholarly Context

Pamela Smith showed that Paris universities integrated alchemical and medical knowledge as part of the broader natural philosophy curriculum in *The Body of the Artisan* (2004) ch.4.

c. 1404 CE, Paris: The University of Paris maintains its integration of Operational Chemistry and natural-philosophy into the natural science curriculum, with generations of Parisian scholars having studied and taught these disciplines. By the early fifteenth century, alchemical knowledge is so thoroughly embedded in university natural philosophy that it is routinely discussed alongside discussions of Aristotelian natural science, medical practice, and pharmaceutical preparation. The comprehensive integration of alchemy into Parisian natural philosophy establishes Paris as the intellectual center for the study of alchemy in medieval Latin Europe.
