Scholarly Context

Pamela Smith demonstrated Antwerp's printmaking and painting workshops were centers for transmitting embodied alchemical knowledge in *The Body of the Artisan* (2004) ch.5.

c. 1512, Antwerp: The early 16th century found Antwerp definitively established as the Low Countries' preeminent center for alchemy and chemical practice. The city's role in the spice trade, pharmaceutical commerce, and luxury goods production created unprecedented demand for expertise in Distillation and chemical-experimentational techniques. This moment marks the beginning of Antwerp's century-long dominance as the capital of European practical alchemy.
