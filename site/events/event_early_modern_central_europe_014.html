c. 1584 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

Prague

Bohemia

c. 1584, Prague: Under Emperor Rudolf II's patronage, Prague becomes the supreme center of European alchemical and natural philosophical research, with laboratories, libraries, and collections of remarkable diversity attracting the era's most accomplished practitioners. Prague's transformation into a center of cosmopolitan learning under Rudolf II established institutional conditions for systematic chemical research. Rudolf's court became legendary across Europe for its support of alchemical research, drawing practitioners from across Christendom and the Islamic world.
