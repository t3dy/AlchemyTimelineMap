Scholarly Context

William Newman established that early modern London housed a vibrant community of practical alchemists and chymists engaged in experimental programs in *Gehennical Fire* (1994) ch.5.

c. 1548, London: Under Edward VI's Protestant government, alchemy undergoes both intellectual refinement and institutional pressure. English scholars increasingly frame Operational Chemistry as a form of natural philosophy compatible with reformed religion, emphasizing the empirical observation of chemical transformations over claims of spiritual enlightenment. This Protestant inflection of alchemy establishes a vocabulary of systematic experimentation that will later enable the Royal Society's legitimation of chemical study.
