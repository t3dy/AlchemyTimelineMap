Ray (Rhages)

Persia

Scholarly Context

William Newman and Lawrence Principe demonstrated that Islamic alchemy synthesized Greek and Indian traditions into a sophisticated experimental science in *Alchemy Tried in the Fire* (2002) ch.2.

c. 1144 CE, Rayy: The advanced state of alchemical expertise in Rayy during the twelfth century reflects the discipline's evolution from early experimental ventures to sophisticated natural philosophy. Practitioners possess detailed knowledge of Distillation, Sublimation, and Calcination, supported by generations of accumulated technique. Rayy's alchemists maintain laboratories with specialized equipment, pursue investigations into the transformation of matter, and preserve textual traditions dating back centuries. This level of sophistication will eventually inspire medieval European alchemists seeking to recover and master Islamic chemical knowledge.
