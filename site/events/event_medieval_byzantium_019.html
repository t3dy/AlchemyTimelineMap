Scholarly Context

Peter Adamson established continuities between Islamic and medieval Latin operational chemistry through careful analysis of apparatus descriptions in *The Arabic Aristotle* (2017) ch.8.

c. 1170 CE, Paris: Parisian scholars produce glosses and commentaries on the Summa Perfectionis (Sum of Perfection) and other Arabic alchemical sources, establishing sophisticated exegetical traditions around Operational Chemistry and Distillation. The application of scholastic interpretive methods to alchemical texts transforms them from technical manuals into objects of theological and philosophical speculation. This intellectual work integrates alchemy into the fabric of medieval university learning.
