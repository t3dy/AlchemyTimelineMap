Scholarly Context

Paula Findlen demonstrated that Vienna's imperial court patronage supported alchemical research as part of the Kunstkammer tradition in *Possessing Nature* (1994) ch.6.

c. 1518, Vienna: The polymathic philosopher Giordano Bruno, whose radical cosmology and embracing of infinite worlds positioned him as a critical intellectual figure of the sixteenth century, pursues investigations into the nature of matter and transformation during his wanderings through Central Europe. Bruno's engagement with alchemical texts, including the Kitāb al-Ḥāsib, occurred within the broader context of his attempt to reconcile Hermetic philosophy with cutting-edge natural philosophy. Bruno's defense of alchemical possibility against mechanical materialism profoundly influenced the philosophical defense of transmutation theory in the early modern period.
