Bruges

Low Countries

c. 1408, Bruges: Intellectual networks connecting Bruges to Italy brought renewed interest in the philosophical frameworks of Pico della Mirandola, whose synthesis of Cabala, Neoplatonism, and alchemical theory shaped Renaissance alchemy north of the Alps. Bruges' role as a international banking and manuscript center ensured rapid dissemination of such ideas among wealthy clerics and merchants. The city's alchemical activity during this decade, focused on Distillation and chemical-experimentation, emerged from this dual foundation of commercial sophistication and humanist learning.
