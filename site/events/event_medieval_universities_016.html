Scholarly Context

Mordechai Feingold showed operational chemistry was integrated into university curricula and natural philosophy teaching in *The Decline and Rise of European Jewry* (1992) ch.5.

c. 1392 CE, Bologna: Bologna's faculty continues to maintain extensive collections of alchemical manuscripts and teaching materials, including translations and commentaries on Jabir ibn Hayyan's works and other Islamic sources, making the city a center for the study of Operational Chemistry and natural-philosophy. The city's position as a commercial hub with access to Mediterranean trade networks ensures that Bologna's scholars have access to the latest manuscripts and translations circulating in European intellectual networks. Bologna's institutional commitment to collecting and preserving alchemical texts ensures their availability for future generations of scholars.
