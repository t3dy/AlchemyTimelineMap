Scholarly Context

Paula Findlen showed northern European courts and mercantile centers collected alchemical manuscripts as part of broader Kunstkammer traditions in *Possessing Nature* (1994) ch.8.

c. 1672, Antwerp: The later 17th century marked the consolidation of the transition from alchemy to chemistry as the dominant framework for understanding matter and transformation. Antwerp's earlier role as the capital of European alchemy became historical memory, though the city maintained important practitioners and collections. The systematic knowledge of Distillation and chemical-experimentation survived this transition, now integrated into emerging empirical chemistry rather than embedded in hermetic philosophy.
