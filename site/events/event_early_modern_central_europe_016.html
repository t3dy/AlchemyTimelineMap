Prague

Bohemia

c. 1596, Prague: Rudolf II's Prague represents the culmination of imperial patronage for alchemy, with dozens of accomplished alchemists, metallurgists, physicians, and natural philosophers working in laboratories seeking to achieve transmutation and the stone of the philosophers. Prague's international alchemical community of the 1590s consolidated transmission knowledge from medieval Islam, medieval Latin scholarship, and Renaissance Hermeticism. This concentration of resources and talent established Prague as the intellectual capital of early modern alchemy.
