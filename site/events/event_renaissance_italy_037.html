Venice

Italy

c. 1596, Venice: The Venetian physician and natural philosopher Giambattista della Porta publishes his definitive work on Transmutation and natural magic, synthesizing decades of alchemical experimentation with hermetic philosophy and empirical observation. His work represents the mature fruit of Venice's pragmatic, merchant-driven approach to alchemy, treating Operational Chemistry as a rigorous empirical discipline grounded in careful observation and repeated experiments.

Concepts

  • Quintessence
  • Operational Chemistry
