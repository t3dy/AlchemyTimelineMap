Bruges

Low Countries

c. 1584, Bruges: The later 16th century found Bruges economically marginalized but culturally significant, with the city maintaining an important collection of historical alchemical texts. The continued interest in Distillation and chemical-experimentation among Bruges' clergy and educated merchants reflects the tradition's deep roots. As Antwerp's dominance reached its peak, Bruges' preservation of earlier knowledge ensured continuity with the Low Countries' longer alchemical history.
