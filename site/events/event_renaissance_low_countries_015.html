Scholarly Context

Allen Debus showed the early modern period witnessed the gradual transformation of alchemy into chymistry and chemical medicine in *The Chemical Philosophy* (1977) vol.1 ch.1.

c. 1520, Bruges: The early 16th century saw Bruges increasingly dependent on its historical collections and reputation as scholars and merchants migrated toward Antwerp. Nevertheless, alchemical studies continued, supported by wealthy clerics and intellectual traditions spanning generations. The city's engagement with Distillation and chemical-experimentation persisted, though Bruges' role shifted from center to periphery of Low Countries alchemy.
