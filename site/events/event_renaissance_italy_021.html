Scholarly Context

Leah DeVun showed transmutation was pursued within mystical and theological frameworks by medieval and early modern alchemists in *Prophecy, Alchemy, and the End of Time* (2020) ch.4.

c. 1468, Venice: A Venetian scholar produces a Greek-to-Latin glossary of alchemical terminology drawn from the Liber XXIV Philosophorum (Book of Twenty-Four Philosophers), explaining technical terms in Operational Chemistry and Transmutation for a readership lacking direct access to Greek texts. This lexicographical project demonstrates Venice's commitment to rendering Islamic and Byzantine alchemical knowledge accessible to Latin-reading practitioners and intellectuals, facilitating the transmission of practical protocols.
