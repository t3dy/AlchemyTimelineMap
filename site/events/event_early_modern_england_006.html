c. 1536 CE Early Modern (15th–18th c.)

London

England

c. 1536, London: Copies of the medieval alchemical treatise De Sex Rerum Principiis (On the Six Principles of Things), a foundational text on the metaphysical principles underlying all transformation, circulate in English university libraries and private collections. The six principles framework (matter, quantity, quality, substance, form, and necessity) provides English scholars with a rigorous theoretical vocabulary for discussing how Operational Chemistry can effect real changes in substances without invoking purely magical agency. This text becomes essential to the Anglophone philosophical vocabulary of transmutation.
