Scholarly Context

Michael McVaugh demonstrated that iatrochemical transmutation goals were pursued in medieval pharmaceutical contexts in *The Rational Surgery of the Middle Ages* (2006) ch.2.

c. 1680, Prague: The De Sex Rerum Principiis (On the Six Principles of Things), a medieval compendium of alchemical theory on the six principles underlying all material transformation, continues to structure Prague's theoretical approach to laboratory investigations into transmutation. Prague's continued reliance on medieval philosophical frameworks demonstrates the persistence of early modern alchemy's integration with scholastic natural philosophy. This text's enduring authority in Prague shows the deep continuity between medieval and early modern chemical thought.
