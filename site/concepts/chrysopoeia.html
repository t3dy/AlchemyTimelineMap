Chrysopoeia (Gold-Making)

Category: ACTOR_TERM

Chrysopoeia (Greek: gold-making; also argyropoeia, silver-making) was the practical goal of producing gold or silver from base metals through alchemical operations. This is an ACTOR_TERM used in Greek alchemical literature to designate the art of metallic transformation, distinct from the spiritual or philosophical dimensions that later accretions added.
