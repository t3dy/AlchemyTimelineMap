Philosopher's Stone

Category: ACTOR_TERM

The Philosopher's Stone (Latin: lapis philosophorum) was the central goal of Western alchemy: a substance believed capable of transmuting base metals into gold or silver, curing all diseases, and conferring longevity or immortality. This is an ACTOR_TERM — historical practitioners explicitly sought this substance and would recognize the concept under this name.
