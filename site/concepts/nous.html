Nous (Divine Intellect)

Category: ACTOR_TERM

In Hermetic and Neoplatonic philosophy, the divine intellect or supreme mind. In the Hermetic cosmology, *nous* mediates between the transcendent and the material; knowledge of God is always mediated through the divine intellect.
