Nous (Divine Intellect)

Category: ANALYST_TERM

Neoplatonic philosophical concept of divine intellect or mind permeating reality. Medieval and early modern alchemists understood nous as the principle enabling transmutation: accessing divine intellect through alchemy meant participating in creation and transformation. Central to Hermetic philosophy and Neoplatonic alchemy.
