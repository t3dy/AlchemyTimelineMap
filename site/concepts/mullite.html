Mullite

Category: ACTOR_TERM

An aluminum silicate ceramic (Al₆Si₂O₁₃) created when kaolinitic clay is fired above 1,300°C. Medieval Hessian crucible makers unwittingly synthesized mullite, achieving superior thermal and chemical resistance. Formally identified in 20th century; medieval mastery was empirical.
