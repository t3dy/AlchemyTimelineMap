Paracelsian Alchemy

Category: ANALYST_TERM

Reorientation of alchemy toward medicine and pharmacy by Paracelsus and followers, abandoning metallic transmutation goal in favor of pharmaceutical preparation. Emphasized empirical observation, chemical preparation of medicines, and integration of alchemy with medical practice. Transformed alchemy from esoteric to practical medical tool.
