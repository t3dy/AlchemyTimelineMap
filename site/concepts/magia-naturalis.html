Magia Naturalis (Natural Magic)

Category: ACTOR_TERM

The practice of manipulating natural forces through understanding of hidden correspondences and sympathies in nature. Distinguished from demonic magic by practitioners as working through natural principles rather than supernatural intervention. Intellectual framework linking alchemy, astrology, and empirical investigation.
