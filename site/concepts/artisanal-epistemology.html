Artisanal Epistemology

Category: ANALYST_TERM

Knowledge embodied in bodily practice, sensorial engagement, and craft mastery. Rooted in hands-on experimentation, tacit understanding, and apprenticeship transmission. Pamela Smith argues this epistemology was foundational to the Scientific Revolution.
