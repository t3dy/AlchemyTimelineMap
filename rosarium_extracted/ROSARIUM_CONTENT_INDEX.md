## Part 1: Alchemical Operations & Processes

### Mercury (118 mentions)
**Database status:** Part of `sulfur-mercury-theory` concept; individual "Mercury" concept may be warranted

**Key themes in Rosarium:**
- Mercury as universal solvent ("no fountain nor water found like unto me")
- Mercury as the prima materia or starting substance
- Mercury's dual nature: liquid metal and volatile spirit
- Argent vive (quicksilver) as fire: "Know therefore that Argent vive is fire and burns bodies more than fire"
- Mercurial principle in bodies: "We are the beginning and first nature of metals"

**Proposal:** Create standalone **Mercury concept** (if not duplicative with sulfur-mercury-theory):
- Actor term: Mercury/Argent vive (quicksilver)
- Analyst term: The mercurial principle; the volatile, receptive aspect of matter
- Definition should emphasize practical familiarity: mercury compounds were readily accessible, their color changes observable, their behavior mysterious to medieval minds

**Text passages worth preserving:**
- "The juices of Lunaria, Aqua Vitae, Fifth Essence, Spirit of wine, mercury vegetable, are all one"
- Descriptions of mercury's preparation and distillation

### The Rose Garden Metaphor
**Mentions:** Organizing metaphor throughout

**Database status:** Likely referenced in `emblematic-alchemy` or `philosophers-stone`

**Rosarium innovation:** The rose garden is not mere decoration—it structures the entire pedagogical and operational framework:
- Garden preparation = prima materia work
- Cultivation = application of heat
- Bloom stages = color progression (nigredo, albedo, etc.)
- Perfect rose = philosopher's stone
